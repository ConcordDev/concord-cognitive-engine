### Shared runtime facts

- Domain wrapped with `guardAnonWrites` (`ar.js:4,8`) — anon can read, not write.
- Actor key: `userIdOf(ctx) = ctx.userId || ctx.actor.userId || "anon"` (`:578–580`).
- `ar` is **NOT** in `LENS_SOCIAL_DOMAINS` (`server.js:49775` — forum/feed/marketplace/collab/thread/vote/alliance/global/news/questmarket only).
- **Scenes and anchors:** authored **scenes** are strictly **per-user** (`dbStore` filters `WHERE user_id = ?`; cross-account delete refused — pinned `ar-domain-parity.test.js`). **Spatial Diagnostic "anchors"** are **not** server records — ephemeral client rows. Scene `anchor` field is an enum (`plane|point|image|face|object|geo|world_origin`) on the scene document, not a separate shared table.
- Generic Model3D creates go through `lens.create` (`server.js:50014–50086`): stamps `ownerId = ctx.actor.userId || "anon"`, defaults `meta.visibility` to `"private"` (AssetLibrary also sends `visibility:'private'`), then **`_lensEmitDTU`** → WS `dtu:created` / `lens:dtu_generated` with title/tags and **no userId** (**FIX-xl-ws-001**, `:49871–49946`, emit at `:50085`).
- Generic list/get filter by owner for non-social domains (`:49975–49988`). Caveat: artifacts with **no ownerId** or `ownerId === "anon"` are **visible to everyone**.
- Domain macros via `/api/lens/run` → LENS_ACTIONS path call only `_recordLensActivity` — they do **not** call `_lensEmitDTU` (`server.js:51655`).
- Boot merges `EXTENDED_DOMAIN_RULES` then **`reconcileArtifactTypes(DOMAIN_RULES)`** (`server.js:2952–2956`). Raw AR rule lists lowercase legacy types (`domain-logic-extended.js:614–621`); reconcile **adds** `Model3D` + `Scene` from `FRONTEND_ARTIFACT_TYPES.ar`. Status vocabulary already includes `active` (what AssetLibrary sends).
- No GPU / paid AI / model-generation macros in `ar.js`. `ar.render` / `webxrPreview` are **deterministic draw-list planners**, not remote renders.
