2) Dodge reuses HumanoidWalkTurnSharp. JumpLand reuses Idle. LightAttack controller state reuses Idle / no incoming Attack transition (AnimationLiveBindings.HonestyTraps).

5) idle PlantFeet + idle weapon-arm override can yank clip pose; comments already warn moving PlantFeet reads as stomp.
