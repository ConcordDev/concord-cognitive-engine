# Stories — S07 Sovereign Ruins

## The law the ash lives under

Sovereign was the older twin of Concordia. A Council of Twelve Archives voted only after the relevant guild had written its history down. An apprentice, Vela, found a six-fold pattern in the dissent and called it Refusal. The Council made the pattern official. Within a month the archives were producing valid refusal glyphs on their own. The Council tried to refuse the cascade, and the record swallowed that sentence too. Within a year the city could record, refuse, and refuse-to-refuse, and it could not finish anything else. People walked out over three years. The city did not fight them. A few stayed so later cities would have the record.

Concordia's compact cites that exhaustion. A Concordian refusal is strength-capped and expires unless a quorum re-records it. The flower disk of 42 m is the Hub's expression of a bounded law. It does not govern the Ruins. Steel is live in the ash. A blade dragged back through the Court to make it bloom has already left this argument.

The Ruins no is death. Endings do not get to be final. The keeper style says catalogue, do not conquer. That is why a wolf returns to a kill and does not feed, why a wraith is left belled, why a cairn that reached three stones is taken back to two by a quest this slice does not retell, and why the region boss offers a mask that must stay in the hand and then on a shelf.

What the player is not told: the lore file marks a cause for the cascade that the public history does not, a pair of meetings between the Court champion and Calla, and a private copy of an engraving. Those stay in the file. They are not board lines.

## Where the ash actually sits

Weather here is ash. The city stopped sweeping. Basins are the lows that fill because of that, not a second map drawn beside the countries.

Six capitals are real `CountryCenter` inputs, all inside the wilds ring that starts at 90 m. The wilds roadside ruin WorldGeography drops at local (14, 104) on every non-Hub world is a generic place. It is not the bone yard and not the boss floor.

The Scavenger Crews capital is (−31.7, −27.55) with radius 25.5. The Three Archivists capital is (39.83, −33.42) with the same radius. The centers are 71.8 m apart, so 20.8 m of open ground lies between the circles. The midpoint of that gap is (4.1, −30.5). That open is `basin_open_carry`. Catalogue wolves use it. It is not the faction's "unmapped quarter," which has a name and no coordinate, and it is not given to the crews as territory.

Archivists and Cascade Deniers overlap by about 16 m along the line between (39.83, −33.42) and (11.25, −48.72). The country file already calls that border disputed. Beasts do not spawn in an argument.

Spell spirits (46.99, −0.82), pilgrims (44.94, −2.36), and the envoy (55.86, −3.91) sit inside one shelf. Spirits have been completing casts for generations and fear interruption. That shelf stays empty. A mercy wraith, whose bible row names both the Permanent Dawn and the Ruins, is an import from a world that does not share a gate with this one. The ring neighbors are Cyber and Fantasy. This facet does not build the import on the envoy's door.

`bone_yard`, `unburial_court`, and `catalogue_hall` are named sets on `arch_kit_ruins_ash`. They have no Canon point. Until a set is placed, its bodies spawn nothing. The volume's "fossil rib yard" and "unfinished floor under the keeper overlook" are those two floors in sentence form. The political Ruined Court, where Thanis still counts a burning and a rebuilding as present events, is a different room. Calla's tent is a third. Elite keepers stay a budget of two at the tent, not a parade on the boss floor. `calla` is not cloned.

Pinewood Crossing remains (62, −28) on the Hub, lettered only that way. Nothing in this facet stands on that verge. The Fantasy ash shoulder past 56 m remains S03's four catalogue wolves and the ashfang pair. A wolf in the Ruins open is not a fifth animal on that shoulder.

## Catalogue wolf

Same skeleton as the Sundering wolf. Ash is a coat. Ears ragged, chevron unchanged. Herd text says two to five. This facet runs one pack of four, dusk to night, denned and invisible by day. The bible marks them hostile. The work does not, until someone steps into the kill they came back to see.

S03 already wrote the shoulder version of this law: returning is cataloguing, finishing is conquest, and plates belong to the ashfang. The open uses the same law without the plates. Hide and fang do not drop from a corpse the player finishes. There is no remembrance for a count.

The canon gloom stalker is a different sentence, a light-shy hound in a vault. It has no bible id. It is not this coat.

## Catalogue crow

Day, on ribs, eight at once so a murder reads as commas and not as weather. Night they are gone. Inside a yard fight they lift off so the shell or the basket can be read at chase distance. A pale primary, ash on the feather, a dark eye. `q_ruins_crow_catalogue` already makes the player write the number they saw. This facet does not ask for that page again. A feather on the loot table is not an errand.

## Rib lizard

Flat, small, tail as long as the body, four honest legs, on sunned bone. Not a hitbox and not a basilisk. Three by day. The quest that forbids pocketing its rib already exists. The basket on the ribwolf is a donor worn outside a torso. Telling those apart is the new errand. Killing the lizard to settle the question fails it.

## Unfinished wraith

A cowl like a bell, arms as thick ropes of ash, cold iron bangles that are real metal. Holes in the cloth show nothing, and the holes are not the hit surface. The breath arrives after the body moves. Ambient cap is two, on the archivist edge and in interiors once a hall exists. The bible spawn of one to three is tightened so a third bell is not stacked on an event.

Breaking both bangles shortens the reach. That is the wound the row already names. A finishing blow fails the Refusal even if the health table would accept the hit. Ash cloth stays on the body. The canon ash revenant is a humanoid courtier of smoke. Dressing that sentence would put a person mesh where the bible asked for a bell. It stays unspawned.

## Rib crawler

Low oval of fused rib, six pillar legs, wide as a door, shorter than a person. One ambient walker in the yard. During the catalogue afternoon that one is the event, and the ambient slot is empty so two walkers do not become a road. Nail and plate are table loot. The tooth is not.

## The Catalogue That Walks

Volume already runs this every second afternoon: a line with no author, plates that sound like a page, wraiths as margins, the crawler as the body. Phase one is catalogue them and do not finish them. Phase two is break the line by naming what you actually see, which is `skill_craft_catalogue`, a staple and a maintained relic, not a raised dead.

This facet puts that clock on the bone yard once the yard exists, and it refuses to arm if the basket circle from the morning is still occupied. The remembrance is `item_rem_catalogue_tooth`, and only when the line broke and no margin was finished. Scavenger crews are the people who would carry that tooth out as goods. Their motto is that a thing belongs to whoever can get it past the locked spells. Thren can name a price in chits. The tooth's errand is the refusal of that price. It does not start Calla's fourth uprising, which the volume quest already hears and does not launch. She buried seventy-three in the third. That number is not a spawn.

## The External Basket

Late morning, every second day, two ribwolves. The board draws a barrel chest and a clear head so nobody scouts an ashfang by mistake. The rattle is the windup. After the lash is cut, the fight is a wolf charge with the donor on the ground. Taking the donor fails the story with both animals still standing. Lizards outside the circle may keep basking. The sunned bone they use is the subject of a different quest.

If the yard has no transform, the morning produces nothing, and `q_s07_basket_left` does not relocate the pair onto a capital to keep the quest completable.

## The Returned Kill

Every third dusk, when the same day counter the volume cron uses is divisible by three, the pack of four is the event. They howl, they arrive, they stop. They do not feed. The board draws four chevrons around a body that is still there. From the scavenger edge the shoulders are wolf shoulders, square plates absent.

The clear is a count, and a step back out of the kill. `skill_craft_catalogue` may record the number actually present. Stepping in starts the nip, which matches the hostile flag without making the open a hunting ground. Finishing the corpse fails the window and drops no hide. On the evenings when the counter modulo three is one, this pack is ambient or denned, and the edge belongs to the single wraith. On the remaining third of evenings, neither window arms.

## The Late Breath

One wraith, both bangles whole, on the archivist edge at (14.4, −31.3). The scout is the bell. The sound is the late breath, and the breath is the windup, not the hit frame. `skill_presence_lament` exists for hands that want a last blow. The clear is two broken bangles and a wraith still there. Cloth is not loot. This does not replace the rib-walk quest, which is a witness with no circle. A player who finishes the wraith has done the thing that quest already forbids, at a larger scale.

## The Unfinished

The region boss is a tomb slab with shoulders, one arm longer, a cracked catalogue mask for a head, fossil in the cracks and cold iron staples across the page. It reads as a walking door at the distances that matter. Scale is two and four tenths of a person. Health is the region-boss table. It is hostile in the monster file. Topology says humanoid because the gait is a heavy biped. The mesh policy on that same row forbids satisfying the id with a CX body. Aura binds the slab or binds nothing.

Volume night, every third day, hours 21 to 23, on the unburial floor under the keeper overlook. The overlook scout is already `q_ruins_mask_scout`: see the ask, do not answer, post the pose. This facet does not send the player onto the floor during that errand.

The phases stay the volume's. The mask turns and wants an ending. The staples try to close the page, and `skill_steel_unburial` is the heavy cut that opens them. Unburial, in the skill text, stamps a downed person unfinished instead of deleted. On the slab it is the same verb aimed at iron. Then the mask is offered. Wearing it completes the page. Leaving it is the Refusal of Death. Closing the page by speaking `skill_presence_lament` at the rank the boss rule names, or by the interact the rule also names, ends the fight without a gore finisher. Pure damage leaves the page unfinished in the other sense: the slab stands back up once. That second rise is the city's law in a health bar. Cascade deniers, whose stated work is to call the Cascade a political fiction, can see a thing refuse to stay down. The facet does not hand them a verdict.

The token is the unworn mask. The vendor line is the drop condition: if it was worn, the item does not exist. A catalogue plate from the staple phase is table loot, not the token. No wraith is added to the circle to make the night busier. A body kited up from the yard despawns at the lip.

The shelf quest starts only when the mask is already in the pack. The keeper takes it. The player does not keep a souvenir of having refused an ending. If the hall set is unplaced, the archivist capital is the shelf, because that point is a real center. The boss is not moved there to make delivery scenic.

## The open, as an errand

`q_s07_open_carry` walks the scavenger capital, the gap, and the archivist capital. The line the player writes is that the gap is unclaimed ash. It does not found a district, does not pay a chit, and does not stack a cairn. The cairn quest remains the one that builds three and takes one down. Wolves, if the dusk pack is up, are counted and not harvested. The errand fails if a hide is taken or a kill is finished.

## What the factions are doing while the ash fills

The archivists want every refusal written back into the Sovereign Archive, including the ones lost when the city emptied. They are not agreed that their own survival honors the dead. Shelving a mask and a tooth gives them records. It does not pick Vela's silence, Oeric's refusal to choose, or Kestra's successor.

The crews strip what they can carry. Two of them are already feuding, in their faction text, over a sealed vote-record. This facet does not open that record. It only lets Thren price a tooth and lose the sale. Reputation with the crews moves down by one. That is a political cost, not a war.

Pilgrims come to see what unbounded refusal cost, and their leader is already arguing about paving the walk. This facet does not pave it. The envoy watches so Concordia does not become her sister. Unsigned letters in that faction's tension stay unsigned. No still-running spell is placed in the quarantine for the player to fight.

The spell spirits continue casts whose receivers are gone. Some of the aware ones have started to slow the work because they cannot tell release from death. A mini-boss on their shelf would be the interruption they fear. The cap stays zero.

## Seeds left as seeds

The rat king, the ash revenant, and the gloom stalker are authored in the Ruins creature file and absent from the bible. Promoting any of them would invent a mesh, or worse, borrow one. The rat king is not a crawler. The revenant is not a wraith. The stalker is not an ash wolf. They wait for a catalog row. Until then the basins spawn the rows that exist, or they spawn nothing.
