# Stories — S06 Tunya

## How the ground is actually laid

Tunya's kingdoms are older than the pins that can hold them. `countries.json` drops twelve claim centers into one local plane, each with a radius between 18 and 28 meters. Dinye Gate and Nil Threshold are 26.9 m apart and their circles swallow each other. Aekon Glacier Gate and Sahm Academy overlap the same way, 23.5 m center to center. Asbir Threshold and the ruins entrance overlap. Sahm and Bahiij overlap. Fluxom and the cactem claim overlap. A player who treats those circles as borders will be standing in two kingdoms and in neither. The circles are claims. The walkable facts are the anchors.

Several of those anchors are doubled under one name. Bahiij Crossing is both the capital field at (−48, 28) and the neutral-zone anchor at (−34, 36), 16.1 m apart. Akeia Capital is both (25, 35), which `CountryCenter` will pick, and (39, 43). Cactem Breeder Camp is both (70, 55) and (80, 43). This slice uses the ids. It does not average them into a third, kinder point.

The coordinates are Tunya-local. Fluxom Gate sits at (62, 18) inside that plane. Pinewood Crossing sits at (62, −28) on the Hub. The shared 62 is a repeated number, not a road. Asbir Threshold shares Pinewood's z of −28 and is 134 m of x away even before the world change. Flower Law is the Hub disk of 42 m. A blade at the dye strip is steel. A blade dragged onto the Hub plaza, outside the Arena, is a flower. The Tunya gate on the Hub ring is the plaque at 3π/4. Courtesy there belongs to the Hub slice. Nobody in these seven errands is sent to read it.

What has a name and no pin stays a name. Masond, Corre, Dormas, Vrellan, and Sandrun are factions with districts and no `country_id`. The Hold of First Arrival, the twelfth lip, the sky temple, the quiet quarter, the tide temple, the old Fluxom harbor, and the ancestor caves are the same kind of absence. Schedule files are full of room names — anteroom, shopfront, marketplace, avenue, excavation, pier — and only the pairs listed in the bind notes are allowed to borrow an anchor. Every other room spawns nothing.

`extends_world` on a country row is a label. Asbir and the ruins point at the Crucible's folder name. Fluxom and the strip point at Crime. Nil points at the ruins world. Sangree and Sahm point at Fantasy. Vessine points at Superhero. Akeia and Bahiij point at a frontier string. The animals were not marched along those fields. These quests are not either.

## Four cultures, and the one already told

The harvest law is already told. Fruit, not the tree. Poise if you are not striking. Terrace, shoal, canopy, three windows, no region boss. This facet does not retell the sealie and does not put one in Fluxom's water.

Ark memory is the culture of hulls and of stone that predates the hulls. Seven arks left Earth. Africa's hit first and became, with the people already here, the rooted kingdom of Masond, which still has no claim center. The North American ark came in year 7. Corre walked off it. Asbir stayed by the hull in the southern dunes. Eurasia came in year 14 and split into Bahiij, Dormas, Fluxom, and the interior walk that became Dinye. South America's ark came last and hurt, and founded Aekon. The public rule under all of that is older than any cohort: a wire completes and the current refuses, and a panel in the sun can still wake a terminal because the sun is not a circuit. Pilgrims who want the Hold, the deletion index, or a minted recording already have chains. The errands here stop at a dark door and at three unread marks.

The Bloc is Dinye, Aekon, and Asbir, allied on the sheets and closed by the Dinye Gate Treaty in year 380. Dinye's motto puts the land first and the cities never. The 409 demand asked for registrars, and the council still has one scribe in the anteroom. Aekon's public face is twelve districts, ceremonial and fertile, and a forge on a cold row that has been rationing fuel since the Long Winter of year 412. The Ice Oath of year 200 is the compact's birthday. It is not resworn on this walk. Asbir's public face is the curated tradition and the ark. The reason the three stay locked together is written in the lore as a hidden truth. These errands use the treaty, the demand, the winter, and the oath's date. They do not use the reason.

Radiance and the tide are the public contracts of the non-Bloc courts that have pins. Bahiij keeps joy as a four-generation agreement and a caravan with Sahm that the history file dates to year 200. The corral is on the map. The beast that would stand in it is not in the bible, and a character sheet that says eight elephant mounts does not create them. Sahm's academy is the self-determining seat: a people who left the Masond merger, were not taken by Sandrun, and still refuse a Dormas chair that would edit how arrival is taught. That refusal is already the faction tension. The errand does not relitigate it. Akeia's public law is a tide rite: once a year, a non-Akeia can be written into a provisional approach to the inland councils. The world's posture toward the matriline is curiosity. Curiosity can be walked up to a door. The door in this errand stays shut, and the writ comes back blank.

Harbor and dye are Fluxom's collapse as the public record already tells it. Merchants who fit nowhere else built a refuge, then a duopoly, then a ration, then a sanitation failure, then a sea trade that died. The kingdoms and Fluxom condemn each other, and the lore says both condemnations are correct. The Dye War took four annums. Year 411 left a stone. Year 412 doubled a tax and moved people. The strip is plants. A dye-bug is a sentence in a bestiary this tree does not have as a creature id. A captain who lost a brother in a raid is allowed to stand at the stone. She is not given the war back.

Under those four, the sheets still carry phrases this slice will not perform. Vessine values list ice as memory. Aekon's country row says ice-bloodline and ice magic. Jera's backstory says she forges ice-bloodline blades. Lore's Vessine are healers, and the species correction says healing, not ice. The glacier, the wind, and the fuel ration are the cold that is actually placed. No ice spell is bound, and the phrases are not deleted. Asbir's preferred weapons include a musket-staff. Firearm coverage is not this world's verb. Nobody is armed with one. Sanguire fire is real on the lore page and the only placed pin for it is the Sangree forge, which gets no quest, so a hidden child is not standing next to an objective.

Chair Oren came to the seat in year 411 after a death by natural causes, and nobody contested him. He believes in the Bloc. He does not, in any public sentence, say what the Bloc is paying for.

They stand together there, and the queue past them is empty, because an empty queue is the honest size of a demand that was never staffed. A young walker on the faction list keeps drawing the seven villages into a city. He has no sheet. He is not cast. ## Three marks

The ruins were found again in a drought in year 380. The public sentence is that the original civilization is unknown. Before the Ancient Tunyans there was someone else; the hidden line says they are gone and their walls are not. Aldra's public work is excavation. Her Stratus hour is an excavation site with no pin, so she stands at the entrance, which is the claim center at (−55, −55). Her sheet stores `faction_id` nil. That tag is not a path through the covenant. Nil Threshold stays a closed stone with its own overlapping circle and no new visitor.

She hands over clay and charcoal. The sealed chamber is 27 m from the entrance. The player copies three marks outside it and does not read them. Aldra has a translation that contradicts the creation stories, and she has lost two assistants to exposure. Neither fact is collected. The clay comes back to the entrance. The chamber stays shut. The rubbing is not minted. The Crucible label on the ruins row is not a door.

Masond's high mason can read marks the arrivals cannot, and a young architect came home from one season in Nil with five new ones he would not explain. They are not summoned. Half a memory, patiently unshared, is already their culture. This errand adds clay, not the other half.

## What the grove is still doing

While these seven walks happen, the terrace is still under the harvest law. Laborers and factors stay inside their budgets and keep the ecology errands and the volume list. Harpies still take tools and not trunks. Jackals still want roots. The twelfth morning is still a lip with no coordinate, a harpy in an alpha's role, and a pack, and it is still not Glacier Gate. The pollen wyrm still takes a season from anyone who sleeps through the bloom. The veil stag still plants a ward instead of paying an antler, and it is still not standing at Pinewood Crossing.

None of that is restaged. The politics are the claims, the four public cultures, and the seven refusals to finish a secret the canon has already decided to keep.
