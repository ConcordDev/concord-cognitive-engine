# Stories — S17 Crucible foundry beasts

## The law these bodies practice

The Crucible is the eighth gate, at 7π/4, between the Permanent Dawn and the Grid. Its refusal is completion. The world def says a system that refuses to close can never rest, and the line under that is: there is no ninth. The law on the density table is the working form: if it would end, un-end it. Moth wings stay asymmetric.

Steel is live on this continent. Flower Law is the Hub disk of 42 m. A shard that leaves the Crucible and crosses that disk outside the Arena is a flower. Pinewood Crossing stays (62, −28) and is not a lattice span.

The Witnesses document. Their charter says observe, document, do not intervene. Sael has proposed an amendment that would allow intervention when a drift endangers a player. Orla holds the charter. Pem wavers. That argument is already the Charter Question. This facet puts it on a moth, not on a council floor. Politics beyond that belongs to the next slice.

The Lattice Engineers patch, then write the patch so the federation can adopt it. Their fear, written in their own faction row, is becoming the new drift source. The Drift Cultists treat a contradiction as a door. The Procgen Collective knows it was generated, and fears a patch that finishes it into a despawn. The Meta-Witnesses and the Refugees sit inside the eastern overlaps. Those overlaps are not beast ground.

Year 4 opened on a goodhart alert: a macro-call counter rose without users. The lattice wrote a quest, a Witness found a misconfigured load-test runner, the runner was retired, and the lattice published the resolution as a breakthrough. The habit stuck. Year 5 built seven doors, one per drift type the federation had named. The seventh door is unclassified, because new types arrive faster than names. Six further types have appeared since. One has been promoted. The door is still the door people use.

## Three opens, and the welded east

Country circles come from `countries.json` through `CountryCenter`. Radii are the territory fields: 23 m, and 25.5 m for the engineers.

Witnesses sit at (−62.76, 5.49). Procgen sits at (−24.55, −26.33). Centers are 49.72 m apart. After both radii, 3.72 m of open remains. The center of that open is (−43.66, −10.42). A moth fits. A 4 m foundry module does not.

Cultists sit at (28.29, −17.0). Against procgen the centers are 53.66 m apart and the open is 7.66 m, centered at (1.87, −21.66). One 4 m module would have 1.83 m of air on each side. That is the narrowest gap that clears a module. Wider gaps clear one too. The kit's snap is a foundry bay of two by two modules, 8 m on the 4 m grid, and 8 m does not fit this throat. The kit has no Canon transform, so the module is a recommendation, not a placed building. The open itself is already ground. The Un-Ender asked for a gap floor. This is that floor. The basilisk uses it in the morning.

Engineers sit at (48.72, −36.71), radius 25.5. Against procgen the centers are 74.00 m apart and the open is 25.50 m. Because the radii differ, the capital midpoint (12.08, −31.52) is not the center of the open. The open's center lies on the segment, 1.25 m off that midpoint, at (10.85, −31.34). Two drift masses stand there. The open could hold a module or an 8 m bay. It does not. The masses already have the floor.

Six further pairs are open country and still cap 0. Procgen and meta leave 14.74 m at (5.54, −22.24), the narrowest field that would clear an 8 m bay. Procgen and the refugees leave 32.26 m. The Witnesses leave 47.79 m against the cultists, 55.20 m against meta, 61.83 m against the refugees, and 70.70 m against the engineers. The Witnesses name the engineers as a disputed border. The circles themselves do not meet. Those six fields take no moth, no mass, no basilisk, no ring, and no module. The density table already spent its weights on the three procgen-facing opens. A second population on the wide fields would be a count the table does not have.

The east is one weld. Cultists and meta are 7.44 m apart and overlap by 38.56 m. Cultists cut 20.11 m into the engineers. Engineers cut 25.80 m into meta. Refugees cut 16.75 m into meta and 14.63 m into the cultists, and they clip the engineers by 2.08 m. Beasts do not spawn in a disc that already belongs to two charters. Capitals and the districts at capital plus (8, −6) stay empty for the same reason. The engineer outer rim is 86.50 m. Wilds begin at 90 m. The 3.50 m sliver cannot hold a module. The ring out to 126 m stays empty.

`unend_hall`, `shard_yard`, and `leaning_stack` are names in the lattice kit. They have no transform. They are not stand-ins for these three opens.

## Unclosed Moth

`faun_lattice_moth` is the ambient animal. Dusty scales, one teal vein that stops before the wing edge, wings that refuse a bowtie. Harmless. Herd line in the animal file says 3–10. Density weight is 4. This facet fields 4, at night, on the 3.72 m open, and fields 0 by day.

The vein is the whole story. It lengthens toward the edge the way a Witness log lengthens toward a conclusion. If the pattern completes, the catalog rule is already written: the moth is dead, and the row despawns. It does not attack on the way out. Four dead moths are not a fight that was won.

`enc_vein_short` is hours 21–23 when `day_index % 3 == 2`, and only on a night `enc_unender` is closed. The same four moths are the window. One lattice walker, inside the budget of 4, comes down from the cart and tries to stitch the vein the rest of the way, calling the stitch documentation. The walker is CX, Sword, coat with one unfinished hem, and is not a legal strike. The clear is to stop the vein. `skill_craft_unend` does that. Striking a moth fails it by the despawn rule.

The charter scores this as intervention. Sael would count the saved moths. Orla would count a broken watch. Both of them are `crucible_witnesses`. The clear pays that faction +1 and the collective +1, and leaves the argument in the log. It does not invent a second witness faction.

`q_crucible_moth` already tells a player to reject a corrected bowtie. `q_s17_vein_short` is the other sentence: a finished pattern is a corpse. The volume quest is left as it stands.

## Drift Mass

`mon_crucible_drift` is the canon drift. A teardrop core, three fat shards, plates that are bevels, one teal seam that does not close. The silhouette note is load-bearing: more shards become VFX and do not count as body. Hostile is false in the monster file. Health is the skirmisher table. Spawn line says 1–3. Density weight is 2. This facet fields 2, on the engineer open, and does not field a third to satisfy the spawn line's upper number.

They are the instability between people who write patches and people who were generated and can be patched out. Ambient, they glide. The seam is the hit. The tone almost resolves, then drops.

`enc_two_masses` is hours 11–13 when `day_index % 3 == 1`. The pair is the same two, not two more. Between them a fourth light tries to become a plate the engineers could number. The light stays light. Hitting the seam can move a mass. Hitting the flare, or writing it into a tally, fails the window. Killing a mass drops no `unclosed_shard`.

The first lattice-born quest was a counter that rose without a cause that belonged to it. The seventh door exists because a type arrived that the six names could not hold. The flare is that habit in a body: a remainder that wants a slot. `q_s17_three_plates` asks for the count three, turned in at the shard cart. It is not `q_crucible_unend_shard`, which already handles a shard that wants to be a finished edge.

Orla has a class of alerts the lattice produces only when a player is here. She has not told the other Witnesses. The flare is not that class, and this story does not explain the cause.

## Unclosed Basilisk

`hyb_latticebasilisk` is the foundry beast that can stand on open ground before the building exists. Thick serpent, quartz ribs as a broken cage, teal only in the gap. One body, one donor trait, one hook: a strike that rewinds the target's last dodge if it connected, once per cast. The click is a music box, and only on the rewind. Hostile is false. The morning window sets aggro for that instance. Ambient cap is 0, because the density table does not list it and this slice does not add a weight. Catalog spawn is 1. The window fields 1.

`enc_rib_notch` is hours 8–10 when `day_index % 3 == 0`, on the 7.66 m open. The ribs lean toward a meeting. The notch is the weak point and the thing that must stay. Take the rewind once. Leave the cage short. `skill_craft_unend` on the notch is the clear. A kill fails and drops no `quartz_rib`.

Rina Calvex's master found three alloys that harmonize with the focus-lattice and refused to teach the recipe. Rina rediscovered two. The third meeting is what the ribs attempt. `q_s17_rib_notch` witnesses the notch and leaves it. The quest does not contain the recipe. Cultist standing rises. Engineer standing falls, because a patch was refused. The collective loses standing only if the basilisk is killed, which is a body finished against its notch.

The basilisk is not `echo_serpent` and not `crucible_warden`. Those Canon seeds have sentences and no bible ids. A lag-strike ribbon and a fused-stone quadruped stay unspawned.

## The Un-Ender

`mon_boss_unender` is the region boss. An open ring on three thick polymer legs. The gap is the face. Chrome flecks. Nebula pink only inside the gap. Liquid polymer with a hard skin. Hostile is true. Health is `table:boss_region`. It deletes its own death unless a completion is forced that it cannot refuse. Leaving is a legal victory the world respects, and the monster's own boss rule says the walk-away does not drop the fragment.

`enc_unender` keeps the volume clock: hours 20–23, every third day, cron `0 20 */3 * *`. Phases stay Gap, Close attempt, Almost ninth. Do not fill the gap with a body. `skill_craft_unend` or `skill_steel_shard` on the seam is the close attempt the volume already names. The monster row also writes `skill_craft_lattice_unend`. That string is not the skills catalog id. This facet follows the volume.

The floor is the 7.66 m open. The morning basilisk is gone by the time the ring stands up. A mesh whose footprint is wider than 7.66 m does not clip a country disc to fit. It waits.

`item_rem_unclosed_seam` is the one Crucible remembrance. It drops when completion was forced and the gap is still the face. Walking out stamps refused completion, pays the cultists and the Witnesses, and drops nothing. Closing the night as a ninth Refusal pays both the Witnesses and the collective in the negative, and drops nothing. `closed_ring_fragment` on the loot line is not minted as a second item.

`q_crucible_unender_scout` already sends a player to overlook the gap and post the window. `q_crucible_no_ninth` already walks a path that offers itself as a ninth law and stops. This facet does not restage either.

## The missing numeral

The Compound Mark is a Sere kiln. Soot brick, brand tiles, one fire eye. Its encounter is `enc_compound_mark`, hours 17–20, every third day. The Tessera negotiate afterward. Witness heat, the ledger, and `item_rem_furnace_eye` are Sere sentences. This slice does not move the kiln, does not retune the cron, and does not write a Sere faction delta.

The Unclosed Foundry is the blend: Crucible quartz driven through Crime brass and soot brick, Frontier scrap welcome as scrap, a clock on the gantry missing its last numeral, pipes becoming fiber halfway along a catwalk. Steampunk here is the clock, the pipe, and the valve. It is not a world. The blend's parents are the lattice kit, the Coast kit, and the Frontier road kit. Sere is not a parent. The Mark arrives as a neighboring law because Crime is the ring-road between a furnace belt and a brass bay, and because the Mark's evening and the lattice night share the every-third-day family.

`enc_numeral_open` is hours 17–19 on that same cron, and it ends at 19. Hour 20 is the Un-Ender's on the nights the lattice cron opens, and the Mark's window on Sere still runs through 20. The Crucible face of the bill is the absent numeral. Stamping a digit, bolting a brand tile, or carrying the cooled furnace eye into the bay fails the clear. Nothing in the bay becomes the kiln.

The gantry piece is `glue_clock_face_unclosed`. It has no Canon transform. Until `arch_ix_crucible_foundry` is placed, the window spawns nothing, and `q_s17_missing_numeral` cannot be reached at a capital, on a wide field, or at the bare open. The recommended seat is one 4 m module in the 7.66 m gap. The 14.74 m procgen–meta field would clear an 8 m bay and is left empty on purpose. The recommendation is not a transform. Prop counts for bellows, shard, and forge bench stay the kit's and the density table's.

The seventh door was added instead of stretching the six names. Six new types followed. One was promoted. The missing numeral is that vote, cut into a clock: a face that refuses the digit which would let a foreign bill close it.
