# Sundering — how the realm sits

The Sundering is the world Concordia kept. Refusal barely shows. Life-magic runs without a strength-nine cap, and the tragedies are love that would not stop wanting. That is the lore. The ground this slice will actually bind is smaller and ruder: a lane that must stay walkable, a flower disk that is not this world's law except when you carry steel back into it, and a salt road whose post already has a name.

Granite, moss, timber, gold leaf worn thin. Those four are the whole palette. `#3a6a48` is the moss in the joints. `#c8b48a` is the stone. `#6a5438` is the timber. `#c8a060` is the leaf, and only the leaf. At a hundred meters a hall from `arch_kit_fantasy_sunder` has to read as a pillar and a roof with the color knocked out. Ash is not a second kit. Ash is soot worked into the same mortar, and the gold on an ash collar is painted shut so nobody can mistake it for the curse opening in a wound.

## Two cultures, one stone

Grove culture is the Circle's public face. The forest remembers what the cities forget. Wards are CX bodies in moss leather, one stitch allowed to glow, never a body of light. They turn hostility inward because that is the world law, not because they are gentle. A ward who wins by becoming the thing in the roots has already lost the only fight the Sundering scores.

Ash culture is what the curse did to looking. The Quiet Grove went silent in a night in Year 0. The trees stayed alive and the land did not. Thorne walks it once a week and comes out quieter. The bog, in Year 02 of the Thorns, took forty paces and the village of Greenmire came off every map. Maeris came out of that growth unchanged. On the northeast margin the wolves are the same animal as the pine pack with the coat gone grey. S03 already capped them and already told the player not to file them as a Ruins catalogue. Ash people do not heal that quiet by filling it. They leave the blank where the blank is.

The Circle's schism is the politics between those two ways of seeing. Half of them want Thorne home. Half want him in the ground. Both halves know the curse started inside the Circle. This slice does not say who released it. That name is his, and it is not a quest reward.

## The map that was dropped on the Court

Eight factions already have rows, mottos, currencies, and rivals. `countries.json` also gave each of them a gate and a district point, plus a territory radius of 25.5 m. Measured from the plaza, those points are not a continent. They are a ring of names pressed onto the same field as the flower disk and the north lane.

The pantheon temple point is the dangerous one. It sits about three meters off the lane center, and the lane's blocked strip is 2.6 m wide, so a body standing there stops the stride the Canon function exists to protect. Nothing spawns on it. Kaelan, when she has a mesh, stands at the pantheon gate instead, still inside the flower disk, with enough clearance that a person is not a wall.

Other points inside 42 m — the glade, the Lit Veil gate, the thieves' gate, the underwarrens point, the university district — can hold a person and cannot hold a steel castle. A paladin crusade, a warren knife, and Corin's edge are flowers there. That is not a metaphor this slice invented. It is the Hub law meeting a coordinate somebody already wrote down.

Points outside the wall are the salt post, the toll a few meters north of it, the league gate, and camp north. The league gate is 120 m from Pinewood Crossing. The salt road is not league land, whatever a radius drawn around a different gate might wish.

The radius itself is not built. If it were, every territory would cover the plaza and each other. The Sundering's politics are a claim that the ground is not obliged to pave.

## Houses, throne, truce

Five Great Houses, one hollow throne. Voss is the only house with a faction row and a gate. Vaelmoor keeps eastern grain and an old grievance. Thornvale holds the iron passes and sells to whoever bleeds. Sereth, dwindling, still owns the river-rights worth owning. Ablon bought a title inside living memory and has not been forgiven. None of those four has an anchor. Thornvale is not Thornwood. The glade anchor is a wildwood trade zone. The keep that stood nineteen days is a siege in Year 03 of the Thorns, and it has no point.

Above them the Crown is weak because the Houses prefer a game to a monarch. Lore names King Aldous Ferrowyn IV, sickly, twenty years crowned and ten years absent from the ruling. The faction sheet names Queen Morwen and an heir the court is in panic over. Both stand. The Held Curse is not her mount and not his. The drake aerie is a district name without a coordinate. No western dragon is placed to settle the motto.

Seraphine and Thorne refused each other three times: a spring ball, a forest under safe-passage, a neutral monastery that was never given a point. After the third they kept an unwritten rule. They do not move on each other directly. The rule has held. An unwritten rule that gets filed becomes a treaty, and a treaty is something a house can break in public. Kaelan's errand exists so the draft goes back unmarked.

The masquerade disappearances are older than Aria's case. Seven guests, four other houses, no verdict, and a smaller echo every year after. Aria has spent thirty years building the case and has not executed it. The public record is enough for this slice. The private mechanism of the house is not a clue the player is handed.

## The salt road

Pinewood Crossing is outside the wall, southwest of the plaza, lettered only with that name. The Sundering's north lane is a different spoke. Linking them is not a tunnel and not a rename. It is a reading: the grove looks at the Hub post from its own side, the stag herd stays the herd S01 already capped, and the milepost griffin stays on the Fantasy side of the seam where the volume left it.

Someone stands eight meters north of the post and calls himself a toll in Thornvale's name. He sells the idea that the salt road is a pass, and that a pass can be paid in blood because Thornvale sells to whoever bleeds. He cannot show a writ. Sereth's river-rights are real in the lore and do not apply to a road that is not a ford. Vaelmoor's grain does not load itself onto a salt wagon because a player walked past. The verge stays a verge.

Killing him would be a small version of the war the league is afraid to start and the Circle is afraid to become. He has no health bar because this slice will not invent the number that would make that killing a system.

## Six errands

**The writ.** The pinewood ward, who is already one person in the guard budget, asks you to read the post and then the man. You come back with the name intact and no signature. The coat quest and the Hub milepost quest are different work and stay on their own boards.

**The blank.** Maeris is not moved to a bog this slice invents. If the crossing she already teaches from has no transform, the errand waits. If she is there, you ask what to write for Greenmire, and you write nothing. Her eight steps, three of them invented each time, are a different kindness. You do not add a village to make the map feel finished, and you do not open the lattice underbrush. That crossing is the other chain.

**The draft.** At the pantheon gate, inside the mercy, Kaelan has a paper that wants to be the three refusals. You hear the three places and you do not visit them. You do not step onto the temple point, because that point is the lane. You do not ask her which prayer has gone unanswered. The truce holds because nobody was brave enough to administer it.

**The nineteen days.** Corin stands in the glade, which is inside the disk, so the sword he keeps sharp is a flower for as long as he tells it. He was knighted at twenty for a cousin of Seraphine's pulled out of a Verge ambush. Fifteen years, no favour. The keep is where her father fell and where she took the wall at twenty-one. You are not standing in that keep. You do not plant nineteen stones, on the lane or anywhere else. You do not go to her gate and spend what he has refused to spend. What he has known for nine years is not your question.

**Both marks.** The same ward tells you the league's public trouble: two warbands, a warchief who has not chosen. You walk to the league gate, far from the salt post, and you leave the board as you found it. Camp north is a point you do not stock. Camp south is a name you do not go hunting. Grokka is not spawned so that the choice can be put in his mouth. A CX body in patchwork hide is the only body the league is allowed, and this errand does not spend one.

**No verdict.** Aria at her gate, flower law still on, hears the seven and the four investigations and the absence of a verdict. She does not gain a page. You do not ride to the crimson quarter to make the thirty years suddenly decisive. The lacquer box stays shut on its own quest. The crypt perimeter stays a name.

None of the six teaches a skill. Curse-fold, the salt draw, and the ward cut keep the grants they already have. Nobody is paid in coin for refusing a war. Faction reputation numbers on the sheets are not edited.

## What the animals already meant

The Held Curse offers the win the Crown would call a weapon and the Circle would call a funeral. Refusing the crest is still the other quest. The basilisk offers stillness, the ashfang pair a plate, the cursebeak a peck. Those are not border incidents between houses. The griffin at the seam is not the toll's hound. The pine wolves are a grove pack. The grey coats are ash, and they are not a claim by the Ruins. The stag on the salt road is not Sereth's herd and not the league's game.

That is the whole martial picture this facet adds: the realm's fights were already authored, and the politics is the refusal to aim them at a neighbor.
