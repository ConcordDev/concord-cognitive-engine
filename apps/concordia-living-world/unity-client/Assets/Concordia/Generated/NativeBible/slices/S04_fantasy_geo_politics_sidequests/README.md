## Scale

`countries.json` gives eight anchors and a 25.5 m radius each. Several of those points sit inside the flower disk, and the pantheon temple point sits on the lane. The radii are claims, not countries you can walk as borders. Unplaced names — the bog, the Quiet Grove, the siege keep, the Verge, four of the five Houses — stay unplaced.
