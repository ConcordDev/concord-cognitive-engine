# Stories — hostility that stays inward

## The wood

The Sundering is the continent Concordia kept for herself. Concord's Link arrives thin. The Sovereign, too proud to follow her on a vacation, barely casts a shadow, so Refusal scarcely caps anything here. What rules is her magic with the leash off: granite, moss, timber, gold leaf worn thin, and love that will not stop wanting. The law the gate actually speaks is narrower than that abundance. The Refusal of Hostility: I will not become the thing destroying me. On the world sheet it is one sentence about poise. In the fold it is a wyrm that wants you to win with the curse.

Steel is live. Flower Law is the Hub disk, 42 meters, and it stops at the Court. A wolf that is dragged back through that disk meets flowers, not a sanctioned hunt. The stride north from the Court, x = 11.2 and a little more than two meters to either side, stays empty out to z = 240 because a hill on that strip has already stopped a walk. Animals belong on the shoulders.

The shoulders that can hold an animal are the hill origins outside the 56 meter wall whose feet do not still sit in the stride. Three near hills fail that test by distance alone: 72° at 48 meters, due north at 52 meters, and 108° at 50 meters. The big mid hill due north at 128 meters is outside the wall, and the builder's one shove still leaves its origin in the stride, so it stays empty too. The other origins, including the due-north hill at 74 meters, are stands. A skirt that crosses the lane is not a place to put a body. The northeast pad is centered at (28, 70). Catalogue wolves use its east half, and only the part past the wall. The pad's west edge lies on the lane, and its south edge is still inside the wall. Pinewood Crossing is a different direction entirely, at (62, −28), and it keeps its own name. Eight meters north of that post, the salt toll is a person, not a den. The pantheon temple on the stride and the wildwood gate inside the wall are the same kind of fact: people or nothing, from the geography slice, and no beast from this one. The Quiet Grove, the fold shrine, the blackroot meadows, Thornwood's hall, and Maeris's bog are real in the lore and have no coordinate on the Court walk. They receive animals when their sets are placed. They do not borrow a hill.

Politics sits under the animals and does not get a second essay here. The Wildwood Circle is split over Thorne Blackroot, who took the root curse into his own skin in Year −2 and has refused, three times, to trade that power to House Voss. The five houses keep a hollow throne weak on purpose. Lore calls the sitter King Aldous Ferrowyn IV. The faction sheet calls her Queen Morwen, and says the heir was born without scales. Both records stand. Neither of them rides the Held Curse. The drake aerie is a district name. The goblin league, the Lit Veil, the university, the thieves, and the priesthood do not get stables in this facet.

## Sundering wolf

One pack, six at the most, dusk into night. Long muzzle, high withers, tail low, dull amber, mud and pine resin in the ruff. At a hundred meters the pack is staggered chevrons, which is how you know it is not a blob and not a fox. They hunt outward. The wood's curse hunts by invitation: use me, and the boar is already dead. The old seed called that invitation a thorn wolf, a vine wall, a burst when the body drops. That seed has no bible id, so the snare is a story Velora can forbid and not a prop the player can drop.

By day the pack is denned and the cap of visible bodies is zero. At dusk it is either on one legal hill origin or in the wardwood oaks, and never in both places, and never inside the Quiet Grove, and never on the four empty hills. Press the pack, or stand in a kill, and the nip is real. Leave the kill and the howl is just the pack talking. Hide and fang stay table names. This facet does not pay them out for the turning quest.

## Catalogue wolf

Same skeleton, ash in the coat, ears ragged, chevron intact. Home is the Ruins, where the law is to catalogue and not to conquer, and where the keepers have already noticed that these wolves return to what they kill. On the Fantasy side they are allowed only on the east half of the northeast pad, four at most, and only once that stand is outside the wall. The west strip of the pad is the lane. The south strip is still inside the wall. The pine pack does not share that margin, and the fold does not receive them.

Returning to a kill and leaving it is the whole behavior. Finishing the corpse would be the Ruins' forbidden conquest and the Sundering's forbidden outward win at the same time. The shoulder plates a person might want off that carcass are not this row. They are the ashfang, and the ashfang is a windowed fight, not a trophy on the ground after a quest. Ruins density is untouched. If both spawners run, four is the Fantasy-side count and the ash roads keep their own.

## Dome wolf

Same skeleton again: paler, ears taller, dust in every crease, no collar. Home is the Frontier, whose gate faces the other way. The Dome refuses shelter, so those packs use the wind. The Sundering is shelter, overgrown on purpose. A dome wolf on the north shoulder would be the wrong refusal wearing the right chevron.

The cap in this world is zero, quests included. A pale animal at Pinewood is salt rime or resin on a Sundering wolf, dull amber still in the eye. The errand that checks the coat spawns no second species and leads no one south to the Frontier. If a binder emits the dome id here, the body despawns and the errand fails open. Frontier weights stay on the Frontier block.

## Wardwood boar

Heavy, one bristle blade down the spine, short hooked tusks, mud plates on the shoulder. Daytime, oak pockets, five at most, off the path. The guard's existing errand is the one that herds a single boar onto the ward path and turns it aside without a carcass. This facet does not add a second path quest and does not raise the herd.

Cornered, it charges and thrashes. It does not hunt the player across a meadow. Hunters who know the wood take the shoulder. A tusk the curse has marked is left in the jaw. The loot hook `tusk_short` is the ordinary hook. It is not a license to prize a marked one.

## Cursefold owl

Broad face, short tail, a rectangle when it glides, a tombstone when it perches. Barred feathers, one pale primary, no rune paint. Three at night, on curse-stones outside the basilisk circle and outside the wyrm's circle, and only after the shrine set exists. The north hills do not grow curse-stones to host them early.

They do not speak prophecy. A scream means something in the fold has already turned. The Quiet Grove, which lost its birds in a night, does not get a consolation owl. A crow from the Ruins is a different id and stays on ash.

## Blackroot elk

The large herd of Thorne's road. Shoulder above a person, antlers a dark thicket of snagged bark, scarred knees. Six adults at dawn on the blackroot meadows, when that set is placed. The approach hills are terrace ground for a walk, not this meadow, so the elk cap there is zero.

The calf has no thicket. The quest that tells you to see it and leave it already exists. This slice does not spawn a calf, does not hang a hide, and does not reuse the salt-road stag to fill the gap. The stag is a tuning-fork crown on the Pinewood verge, a different herd, already capped at five by the Hub ecology.

## Veil finch

A seed body and a rust cap. On this continent the dust in the feathers is pine, not the pollen the same id wears in Tunya. Twelve by day on the legal hill origins and the wardwood edge, none on the lane, none on the four empty hills, none in the Quiet Grove. The northwest pad points at Tunya. Finches may use its Fantasy-side edge. Sealies, goats, and herons may not follow them across to keep the flock company. Tunya's own weights are unchanged.

## Pinewood stag

Salt in the fetlocks, antlers inside the body width so the distant read is a tall wedge. They belong to the seam at (62, −28). The Hub slice already allows five past the wall. Fantasy looks at those five from the grove side of the post and adds none. The milepost griffin, on its own clock, tests the Fantasy side of that same seam and does not move north to be near the wolves.

## Fold basilisk

Thick coil, one diamond hood, moss and resin, a pale eye. The rest pose is a stone. The strike pose is an S. No chicken legs. One, on the fold floor, at the noon the volume already set, every second day. The overlook is where Thorne's scout quest watches the windup and leaves. Watching does not arm the circle.

The hood's offer is stillness. Stillness is the curse using you, the small version of the offer the crest will make with gold. Move while the hood is opening, or commit to the hood-root and mean it. Half a step gets the gaze, and the gaze is that telegraphed flare, not a screen you cannot leave. Killing the brute is allowed. Becoming the statue is the loss. It never joins the wyrm's circle, and the wyrm never calls it in. Scale and fang stay table names. The remembrance scale stays the volume item.

## Ashfang pair

Two wolves with two cracked bone pauldrons each, grown from catalogue bone, not a suit of armor. The head stays readable. They are the border spawn the hybrid row already allowed, one to two, gathered into a single window: mornings, hours 7 to 10, every second day, east margin of the ash shoulder. Outside that window the cap is zero. They are not a trash pack walking wardwood ahead of the boss.

The lunge has a ceramic tick in the growl. The burst opens the shoulders and leaves them bare. That bare shoulder is the tell that the ash was spent. Catching it and wearing it is the curse becoming a tool, which is the loss even if both bodies still have health. The honest hit is the seam on the tick, and the plates stay on the ground. Board, scout, and sound all fire before the circle arms. The bible marks the row non-hostile; the window is what turns aggro on, and only inside the circle. Breaking this pair does not stamp the refuse tag the wyrm listens for.

## Lip cursebeak

A ward griffin's body, one basilisk hood as a leather cowl behind the beak. Grounded, it is still a big cat. The cowl down must still read as a griffin. One, on the fold lip, mid-afternoon every third day, and never on an evening when the Held Curse is in its dusk window. The lip is outside both circles. The milepost griffin is a different appointment on a different road and is not this mesh.

Hawk scream, basilisk rasp, then one peck if you stayed on the line. The peck is short and named. It is not a gaze cone. Leave on the flare, or fold the blow back into your own poise. An outward fold that executes the body fails the story the way the wyrm's outward breath fails it, and still does not count as having refused the crest. Hood-scale and pinion stay table names.

## The Held Curse

The wyrm Thorne will not turn outward. Visible body a little over three times a person's length, the rest in the fold, one crest like a held blade, bark and old iron in the scales. Gold leaks and closes. That gold is the curse trying to be useful, the same gold a ward-blade quest already tells the smith to reject. The crest is the only vertical. There are no wings, no spaghetti silhouette, no saddle.

It stands on the fold floor when the shrine set exists, dusk, hours 19 to 22, every third day, the clock the volume already published. The overlook scouts it. The north hills do not host it. Phases stay as written: crest warn, inward breath, the offer. Break plates to stop the sweep. Stay in the inward cone. When the crest lowers and the sound drops out, refuse. Taking the offer ends the fight as a loss with health still on the bar. Using curse-fold at rank 5 without the refuse tag turns the breath outward and fails the story. Rank 5 itself still unlocks only on that refuse, which the existing fold quest witnesses from the lip without entering the circle. This facet does not replace that quest, does not start the raid from a mini-boss, and does not add wolves, a basilisk, or a cursebeak as help.

Thorne's guest line is the personality: he carries a curse he could turn outward and win with, and he refuses every day. The wyrm is that sentence with a body. It is not Queen Morwen's missing scales, not Aldous's bloodline, and not a mana drake from the aerie. Those seeds and those succession lines stay where they are. Killing the wyrm is allowed. Becoming it is the loss. It does not offer a ride. Ride code is absent, and this row was never a mount.

## Quest — leave the kill

A ward from the existing five, posted on the ash margin and not cloned into a sixth, has watched the catalogue wolves come back to the same carcass two mornings running. The Circle's fear is that the pack is learning Thorne's habit, returning to a wound until the wound answers. The wolves are not cursed. They are doing the Ruins thing on the wrong side of a shoulder: they record, and they go. The errand is to see that and leave. A swing, a looted plate, or a step into the ashfang circle fails it. The crow catalogue on the ash roads is a different quest in a different world, and it stays there.

The backstory the ward will actually say is short. Keepers across the seam have started marking the returns. The Circle will not answer a keeper with a hunt, because a hunt would make the Sundering into the ending the Ruins refuse, and would make the ward into the outward blade Thorne will not be. You are there so the ward has a witness who also did not strike.

## Quest — the pale coat is salt

The other ward, pine verge, same budget, heard a cousin off the salt road describe a pale wolf. The cousin walked as far as Pinewood and turned around, which is as far as a Court errand should go. Salt rimes the fetlocks of everything that uses that road, including the pine wolves when they nose the seam. The dome wolf's pale is a different pale, and it lives where there is no shelter.

You read the post. It says Pinewood Crossing. You stay outside the 42 meter disk. You look at the coat until the amber eye and the pine chevron are obvious, and you report a Sundering wolf. You do not file a Frontier sighting, because a filing would pull that world's packs into this wood on paper, and paper is how the wrong mesh gets spawned. If the dome id is already in the world, it comes out, and the report cannot be completed by leading it home. Home is not this quest's road.

## Quest — count the absence

In Year 0 the grove that was supposed to be the Circle's last sanctuary went silent in a night. No birdsong, no insects, no animals. The trees are still alive. The land is not. The elders will not enter. Thorne walks it once a week and comes out quieter than he went in.

He asks for a count of three absences: finch, owl, elk. A count is a witness, which is what the week needs. A summon, a carcass placed to "bring something back," or any fauna id emitted inside the grove fails the walk. If the grove has no transform yet, the errand waits. Moving the silence onto the north hills would make a living shoulder into a fake catastrophe, and the finches on that shoulder are telling the truth by being there. The calf on the blackroot trail is a different mercy and stays on its own quest. You do not ask him, on this walk, who released the curse. He has told no one. Velora's suspicion stays on her sheet.

## Quest — turn the pack inward

Velora Mossheart trained Thorne. She kept a contingency she has not used, and she will not talk about it on a wolf errand. What she will say is that she has seen a person try to hold a harm in the wood by adding a greater harm, and that the wood kept both. The pack has a boar off the path. The easy picture, the one the unspawned seed draws, is a vine that walls the trail and bursts when the wolf dies. She wants the opposite picture. You spend your own poise: a ward cut if the path quest already taught it, a hail, a fold that comes back into the chest before it goes out. The pack howls and leaves. The boar lives. A dead wolf fails it even if the boar is saved, because the saved boar was bought by becoming the hunt.

This is not the guard's path-clearing. That one turns a single boar with a ward cut and reports the path open. This one is off the path, and the giver is the woman who would have to end Thorne if it came to that, practicing on a smaller mouth. She does not hand you the fold skill. The lip quest already does, when you refuse the crest without taking it. She does not hand you the arrow. The arrow is not a fauna drop.
