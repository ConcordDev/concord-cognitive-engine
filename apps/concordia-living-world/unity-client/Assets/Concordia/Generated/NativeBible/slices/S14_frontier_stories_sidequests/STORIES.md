# Stories — S14 Frontier

The Frontier is the ground between cities, where a sealed thing still has to be carried by someone who can be asked. Canon calls that work peer-to-peer routing, walker journeys, and the human-scale anchors that make trust transitive. The law on the ground is shorter. Drop the dome. Wind does the rest. The road is the door, and a door that grows a roof has failed.

S13 already set the floors where that law is physical: the salt lens where Couriers and Pioneers share 21.54 m, the 18.71 m drift, the pale flat, the cut, the hitch with two led horses. This facet reads the same discs as politics. It does not move a horse.

## The discs

They are 25.09 m apart. The placed camp and the placed ridge are other points, and this facet leaves them empty of these two people. The placed ridge stays at the cap S13 set.

## The post

Ria Hale has run Link Post Alpha for thirty-two years. She was a courier before she settled. She is tired, she names the people she sends, and she keeps the word walker for the autonomous kind. Helva, her grandmother, walked the first parcel in Year 8: eleven days, Concordia to Sovereign Ruins, a Refusal Compact and the accepting reply, a camp where an ankle broke on day six. That camp is the first post. Year 88 the Guild voted to shun the relays. Year 91 the Handshake asked for two signatures. Year 100 a Frontier delegation, Ria's mother and a young Temir among them, watched Concordia ratify the Founding Compact again, and the Frontier became the venue for disputes between cities.

On the hash at (48.72, −9.79) she is present from 5 to 19: dispatch, ledger, afternoon check-in. The cloak is oilcloth. The pin is thirty-two years of cloth and a small metal mark. Her dagger stays in the gear list. After 19 the quarters have no coordinate, and she is gone. Three meters away the safehouse hash stays empty, because Dorvik's night there is marked not interactable, and his daylight hours sit on a safehouse name that is not a district.

She can say the Guild's goal. No peer unreachable more than seven days. She can say the Guild has not commented on three sabotaged relays. She cannot, in this facet, say who did it. That knowledge is the hidden line, and Temir's suspicion without proof is the same line. The errand walks 58.25 m, hears the public debate, and comes back to a silence that is still silence.

She can say the lost parcel as the first public version. Two months ago. Receipt at the post. No delivery log at Sovereign. Dorvik insists. Kestra insists. The Handshake failed once. Both sides are quietly investigating. Kestra has no sheet. Dorvik is not on a pin. The later echo, the second request of the same hash, the addressee the couriers will not read aloud, stays with the militia's known-by list and is not a sentence she speaks here.

She can refuse the question about Lin. Dorvik has begun greeting Lin by name, and Lin has begun greeting him first. The constitution has no provision for an autonomous walker with continuity of identity, so the question has not been raised. Ria will not discuss it. Lin's sheet says `is_conscious` false, a chassis, routes with no district. A chassis is not given a Concordia face so the question can be answered by looking.

## The peers

## The cove

Vexa walked out of a Guild apprenticeship after watching what the Guild ignored in order to keep routes running. The crew is walkaways and Freenodes who lost faith. She has the largest band and the longest list of Guild-marked enemies. The further fact, that the apprenticeship was Ria's and that neither has spoken in twelve years, stays secret. At the cove she is sharp, short, and fair inside her own crew. Morning meeting, evening payout, same pin, one body. The blades on the belt string are not a weapon this facet binds.

The faction tension is the whole quest. Two captains. One sealed message from a Concordia diplomat the Guild was carrying. The dispute may collapse the cove. She does not name the other captain. The diplomat stays a title. The seal stays shut. The ambush corridor, where her midday scout is marked not interactable, stays empty 14.61 m away. The cut stays S13's, including the road bandit whose quest already ends on leaving both cuts unpaid.

## The ridge

Brunn led the North Ridge for fifteen years. He was a Guild post-warden, walked away at thirty, and built the place with a dozen others. He thinks the Web is an addiction. He will not use its jargon. When he must point at it he says the message-line-thing. Wool, canvas, a fur collar, empty hands. He teaches from 8 to 12 and mends from 15 to 19. The lodge between those blocks has no pin.

The public tension is already written on the faction: his grandson has begun carrying messages to the nearest Guild post, Brunn knows, and Brunn has not spoken of it. The secret adds durations and a wait for the boy to confess. The errand uses the public sentence by refusing to ask it in the room. The boy has no id. A child is not stood. The rivermouth hash is two meters away and empty, so the binder cannot split one elder into two. The placed ridge, inside the same two discs, keeps cap 0.

## The temple and the minutes

The glyphs are weave, one dull thread of light, not a body of fire. The staff stays on the list. From 7 to 19 he is at the temple: congregants, then writing, then a study group. The meditation hall is empty because the early hour is not interactable.

Copying it would invent the sermon. The errand leaves the page on the desk. It does not walk to the pronghorn and call a white rump a revelation. S13 already has that flag as a herd.
