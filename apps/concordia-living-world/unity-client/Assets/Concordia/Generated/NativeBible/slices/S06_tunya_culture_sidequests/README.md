## Scale

`countries.json` gives twelve claim centers and radii of 18–28 m. Several circles overlap. They are claims, not countries you can walk as borders. Masond, Corre, Dormas, Vrellan, and Sandrun have faction districts and no `country_id`, so they have no CountryCenter. Schedule room-names that are not paired to an anchor in this slice spawn nobody.
