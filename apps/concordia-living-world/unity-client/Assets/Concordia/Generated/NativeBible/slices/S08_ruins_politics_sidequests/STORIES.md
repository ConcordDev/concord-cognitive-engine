# Stories — S08 Sovereign Ruins

## What the ash is arguing about

Sovereign was Concordia's older twin. For two centuries a Council of Twelve Archives voted only after the guild that owned the question had written the history down. It worked, for a time. In Year 0 an apprentice glyph-reader, Vela, found a six-fold pattern in the dissent and called it Refusal. The Council made the pattern official within a year. In Year 1 the archives began writing valid refusals with nobody asking. The Council tried to refuse the cascade. The record took that sentence too. Within a year the city could record, refuse, and refuse-to-refuse, and it had stopped every other operation. It did not fall in a fight. It stopped doing things.

From Year 2 to Year 5 most people walked out. The city allowed the leaving. A few stayed so a later city would have the record of unbounded refusal. In Year 60 Concordia's founding compact cited that record and bound its own refusals: strength capped at 9, and a quorum re-records the entry within seven days or it expires. The flower disk of 42 m is the Hub's expression of a bounded law. It does not govern the Ruins. Steel is live in the ash. The Ruins no is death. An ending here does not get to be final. Catalogue, do not conquer.

Six factions still act inside that exhaustion. Three archivists keep benches where twelve archives once voted. A scavenger crew carries what it can carry and will not sell a sealed vote before breakfast. A summons keeps calling. A pilgrim leader will not ask for a road. An envoy files the cap and the week as inventory. A priest preaches that the city was abandoned, and he does not open the journals he is known to hold. The court that burned and was rebuilt, and the rebellion that buried seventy-three and is leading a fourth uprising, are in the lore and on people's faction strings. They have no country pin. This slice does not give them one.

S07 already put the animals on the lows where the ash collects, and already put the Unfinished on a floor that is not the political court. This facet does not move them. The open between the crews and the archivists stays unclaimed. A wolf returning to a kill is the archive's law with fur on it. A wraith is a person-shaped gap, not a member of the Spell Spirits. A crawler's line does not become a fourth bench.

What stays in the file and off the board: the cause marked on the cascade, the champion's meetings with Calla, the scribe's private engraving, the archon's other listener, the girl's tavern, the fading of a line, a successor list, a lover in the south camp, a dead recipient, seven unsigned letters, a desk that would end a movement if it were read aloud.

## Where the claims actually sit

Weather is ash. The city stopped sweeping. The countries file did not draw a city to replace it. It drew six claim centers, all inside 56 m of the Ruins origin, inside the wilds ring that starts at 90 m. Five radii are 25.5 m. The deniers use 23.0 m. Every district pin is the capital plus eight meters on x and minus six on z. That is 10.0 m, the same offset six times. A district and its capital are a claim and a second pin, not two neighborhoods with a street between them.

The Scavenger Crews sit at (−31.7, −27.55). The Three Archivists sit at (39.83, −33.42). The centers are 71.8 m apart, so 20.8 m of open ground lies between the circles. S07 named that open and put the catalogue wolves there. The crews' "unmapped quarter" is a name with no coordinate. It is not that gap. The North Camp pin is 10 m from the crew capital, at (−23.7, −33.55). Thren counts there before dawn. Iby reads there after.

The archivists' circle overlaps four others: the deniers by 16.1 m, the spirits by 17.6 m, the pilgrims by 19.5 m, the envoy by 17.4 m. The denier overlap is the disputed border S07 already refused to beasts. The other three overlaps are the archive's claim reaching onto the north shelf. Kestra is not moved onto that shelf. She stays at the archive pin, (47.83, −39.42).

Spirits (46.99, −0.82), pilgrims (44.94, −2.36), and the envoy (55.86, −3.91) sit inside one another. Spirit capital to pilgrim capital is 2.6 m. Spirit to envoy is 9.4 m. Pilgrim to envoy is 11.0 m. Their district pins repeat the same distances, because every district uses the same offset. The still-casting pin and the pilgrim lodge pin are 2.6 m apart. A summons and a man laying out bread can hear each other. That is the shelf. It is not a beast floor, and it is not three separate towns.

The deniers at (11.25, −48.72) touch the scavenger circle by 0.6 m. A rind, not a shared camp. Their meeting house is 10 m further out, at (19.25, −54.72).

The envoy compound's local x is 63.86, z −9.91. Pinewood Crossing is (62, −28) on the Hub. The shared neighborhood of the number 62 is not a shared place. The Hub gate for this continent is at π/4, between Cyber and Fantasy. Nothing in this facet stands on that plaque.

`catalogue_hall`, `unburial_court`, and `bone_yard` remain S07's unplaced sets. The throne, the sanctum, the rebel camp, the war tent, the scribe's chamber, the refused circle, the memorial, the silent library, the first bench, the south camp, the research outpost, the quarantine, the alternative archive, the blessing grove, and the pilgrim path are the same kind of absence. ## Three benches, twelve archives

Kestra was a graduate apprentice the night the city emptied. The Cascade left her seven recovered glyphs and the working Archive. She has spent the years since cataloguing, and she has watched the other two forget. She speaks in indexed sentences. In the morning window she is at the archive pin, and Sennit is with her.

Sennit came three months ago on Pell's run out of Concordia, a Scholars' Guild dropout who got caught on refusal. Kestra has taken him as an apprentice. The sheet's title calls him one of seven. She has not told him that. The other six have no sheets. They do not stand beside him. The errand counts benches: Kestra's, Oeric's, Vela's. The apprentice is a person at the pin and not a bench. The walk to the capital, 10 m, is there to see that nine more votes are not waiting in the ash. She takes the count. She does not take an heir.

Vela sits a first bench in a chamber with no coordinate, in a silence she has held for thirty years, a strength-9 refusal bounded to her death. The field within twenty meters of her suppresses combat and harvesting. Putting her on the archive pin would drop that disk onto a placed anchor and onto the capital 10 m away. She stays unspawned. The cause of the cascade stays in the lore's hidden line. The silence question stays unopened.

Oeric was militia before he was an archivist. He refused to evacuate, and that refusal became the first authored entry. He re-reads it twice each morning in a courtyard the countries file does not place, and he teaches a blade form to no one in particular. This slice takes the other hour the sheet gives him at the archive, midday, when Kestra and Sennit are gone. He says the public sentence once. The line is not recopied. Whether the Archive is fading or he is stays unsaid. Sword is his people's style. The form is not a quest reward.

## The night haul

Thren has run the North Camp for a decade. She did six years on a rival crew before she was old enough to lead, and she splits a haul fairly. The archivists tolerate her because a glyph she can identify goes back. The envoy does not tolerate her, and she will not be managed. Outsiders are "envoy," whatever they are.

The public feud is two crews and a sealed Council vote nobody has opened. Each thinks a third crew has the key. At the count, hours 3 to 7, she will say the seal is not for sale tonight. The errand looks at the packet and leaves it shut. The south camp has no pin. The private half of the feud is not dialogue.

Iby trained briefly under Vela. She was nineteen. She walked back after the evacuation and refused the archivists' stipend. Her readings keep the camp alive, and she has refused to teach them. From 7 to 11 she is on the same pin Thren just left. One binding is older than the visitor. The errand walks the 10 m to the claim center and back, around the mark. The mark keeps running. The unmapped quarter, where her afternoon sheet sends her, stays empty.

## The cast that has not finished

The Long Summons was a Council member's call to another Council member who never came. It has spoken one name once each day for 187 annums. The recipient is dead. The spirit has begun to suspect, and has begun to slow, and has not asked anyone to confirm. Confirmation would finish the cast inside an hour, and finishing would dissolve the spirit. The faction is split between spirits who know they exist and spirits who do not. The ones who know are asking whether completion is release or death, and they are slowing on purpose.

The chamber is unplaced. The still-casting district pin is the floor. The spirit is a thick ash-plaster robe with cold iron at the hem and one dull light at the mouth for the length of the call. Then dark. It carries no weapon. It is not a keeper wearing a glow. If that mass has no native mesh, the pin stays empty and the errand has no one to turn in to. The name is not printed in the sheet. The player hears a call and supplies no answer, and does not invent the syllable.

The Endless Blessing is a benediction with no congregation and a grove with no coordinate. It does not appear. The Silent Keeper is a memorial golem on the archivists' sheet, indestructible by design, speaking one name per blow. Its memorial is unplaced. Striking it is not politics.

## The witness walk

Isen walked his first pilgrimage twenty-two years ago. He was a low-ranking Concordia bureaucrat with a flat and a salary. He went home, quit, and walked back for good. He has led the lodge for nine annums. He explains the cascade slowly to anyone for whom it is fresh, and he asks more than he answers. The faction's fear is that the walk becomes routine, or that Concordia formalizes it. Paving would bring more pilgrims and would spend the discomfort, which is the point. He has been debating the request. He has not sent it.

The placed route is 10 m of ash between the lodge pin and the pilgrim capital. The errand walks it and brings back the ground as it is. The memorial path he walks at midday is a name without a point. It is not paved in the telling. Two and a half meters from the lodge pin, the summons is calling. Answering it fails the walk. The walk does not cross to that pin.

Pell has run messages, books, and survivor accounts between Concordia and the Ruins once a month for fifteen years. In the evening the sheet puts him at the lodge, which is a real pin, so he stands then, and he is not given a letter. Hours marked "varies" spawn nothing. He is the bridge. This slice does not use the bridge.

## The sister watch

Marrin was a mid-career diplomat. She has been envoy chief for six annums because her predecessor died walking the pilgrim path. She has not decided whether the posting was a promotion. In public she stays in the diplomatic register. She drops it with Kestra and with no one else. That evening is real on her sheet and is not staged here, because Kestra's window is the morning and a second meeting would invent a shared hour on a pin neither of them is given for it.

What she will say, as inventory, is the compact. Year 60. Strength 9. Seven days. The errand writes those two numbers on the sheet she already holds and hands it back. The sheet stays in the compound. It is not a glyph, not a DTU, and not a walk to the Hub council through the gate at π/4. The research outpost where she spends afternoons with Clovis has no pin, and Clovis has no sheet. The unsigned letters arguing that the cascade can be repeated under specific conditions stay where she keeps them. The deputy is not named.

The compound is 9.4 m from the still-casting pin and 11.0 m from the lodge. She drafts while a name is called and a bunk list is checked. None of the three crosses. The shelf does not gain a mercy wraith because the claims stack. Superhero is not a ring neighbor.

Her coat is CX cloth in the envoy's blue and brass, on ash plaster. The faction visual prefers an officer's sword and a concealed pistol. The pistol is not bound. A firearm verb is not this slice. One worn brass button is the emissive, not a uniform of light.

## The closed book

Palen's grandfather walked out during the evacuation and kept journals. The faction sheet says Palen has them and has refused to read them for thirty years. In the pulpit he is declamatory, in three-beat lines. The line this slice uses is the motto: Sovereign was abandoned, not collapsed, and the difference matters. His goal is to convince the world that the cascade is a Concordia fiction told against archive government. His fears are a spell that is visibly still running, an archivist testifying, and the pilgrims agreeing the cascade was real. The meeting house receives none of those proofs.

The table has pamphlets. The journals are not on it. The bedroom where the sheet's secret places them has no coordinate, and a forced public reading would end the movement in a day. The errand says the motto back and leaves the table as he set it. The movement continues. The 0.6 m rind where his circle touches the crews is not a raid.

A generated row called Concord Keepers lists him again, with a generated motto and no districts. He is not that faction. Covenant Seekers lists Thanis the same way. Thanis remains the last archon, counting a burning and a rebuilding in the present tense, in a throne room this slice cannot place. Calla remains at a camp this slice cannot place, the fourth uprising unstarted, the volume errand still the one that hears the plan and stops. Seventy-three stay buried. Dawn stays a different quest.
