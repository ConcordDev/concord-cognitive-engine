# Stories — the twelve who will walk, and the ones who will not

The volume file already knew their names and one sentence each. The sentences were true and thin. A cast is not a sentence. It is a limit: how far the person will go, which fight they refuse, and which secret stays on the sheet.

Cross, here, means the Court already seats them. Eight spokes and a waystone are where their work lives. Following is not a summon that empties a stand. The person who walks is one. The person who remains at the mark is still there.

## The child and the mender

Brackish is eleven. The bell-tower is a bed when the weather is kind. The market is a wage. She knows the gutters and she knows Ren Solare and she will not admit the second. The supper that unlocks her is bread from the stall at the stranger price, offered, not chased. The fact she sells after that is something she saw. If the player is unkind in the first hour the sheet already has a thief; this slice does not write a softer child over that road.

She can say a place-name. She cannot be payment for a gate. The milepost errand exists because the salt road was read once already, by Old Seam, and Brackish was not the mouth. Hearing "Pinewood Crossing" and walking back to the tower is the whole bravery available to her. The hitch inside twelve metres of that post belongs to an earlier slice. No stag is called to witness a child.

Old Seam is serious about the goddess, the dome, and bread, and amused by almost everything else. She taught the Oracle before Nesha, briefly. Twenty years of not speaking are still twenty years after the perimeter walk. Quartus is when she is on the ring and Nesha is on the other ring. Measuring that is a quest that already shipped. Closing it is not a companion reward.

Seam and Esha correspond. Seam lost a smaller open table. Esha read the warnings and built around them. That is the first time the warning met someone who listened. The player is not promoted into their post. The lip at the Sere bearing already has no plaque. Walking it again to deliver a sentence Seam has already sent would be a second copy of a finished letter.

## The witness, the warden, the stall

Maren Ashveil has no century because no sheet gave her one. She has a stand, a book, a chain, and ash on the boots that does not climb to the quill. Her job is the sentence in front of her. Asbir keeps the three notebooks. She does not graduate into his case by being useful. When she writes "two guests, one plaza, not a pair," the reason is an inference, and inferences are how embassies eat a day. The Jax betrayal is exactly the kind of sentence an embassy would love. It stays off her page. If the player speaks it, the page is struck and the errand fails. Mama Delgado's sheet gives Jax a cease-fire and information shared never the same kind twice. Vesper's sheet gives Mama a back-channel. Neither document says she already holds the authorization and is keeping it from him. An earlier draft of this slice said she was. That sentence is withdrawn. The player still does not get to be the channel, and still does not get to be the person who tells the stall that the Ghost has Jax's face. The Dawn sheet says Vesper does not know that yet, and that he will, soon. Soon is not this errand.

Gale's life, as far as the sources go, is the disk. Poise, an open helm, a flower in the plaza, an estoc on the sand. The urn quest proved the split. Sending him out past the wall to parry a basilisk would turn a law into a bodyguard. He will look at a crest shard someone else earned and tell them to sheathe it. He will not pretend he saw the blow.

Vesper's Court face feeds people and moves a price when standing moves. The Dawn face, on the other sheet, wants a city saved so it can be owned. Both documents exist. The companion is the stall. Plasma, the mask, the orbital, the finishing blow: none of those are the assist. The pronoun split between the two documents is recorded in the primary file and is not resolved by picking the convenient one in a line of banter. The stall speaks the name.

Jax will not stand in that party. He confirmed, on his own sheet, who authorized the death of half his unit. He also will not take a contract on a child, which is why Brackish's road and his road do not share a verb. Contracts from all eight, loyalty from none, is the guest line. The invoice is the loyalty he does keep: the paper lands, and it is not tucked into a lining to spare a friend. Cipher, the fixer who does not speak, stays unpromoted. A silent logistics net is not a second companion.

## The curse held inward, and the bog that laughs

Thorne's two centuries are on the fantasy sheet. His body, in this bind, is not the sheet's elf. The plate already settled it: CX, tallest guest, wraps, a braid he will not tighten, the curse carried as posture. One green thread at the wrist is as much glow as the art direction allows. The bone beads stay because the sheet says they are what keeps him himself. They are beads, not a trophy rack.

He knows who released the curse into the elder roots. He has not said it. He is afraid that person is still alive, and that a meeting would finish what the curse began. The fear is on the sheet. The answer is not. The bone beads in the braid are what keep him himself. Taking one is the same dismissal as taking the offer. Velora has not spoken, for twelve years, about being the one who would put him down. She carries an arrow she has not loosed, on the condition that he has not killed first. Seraphine asked three times and was refused three times, and the realm's balance is that unwritten rule. None of that becomes a campfire story because the player named him walker.

The riddle Nyx framed is wolves and walls. It was sent. It arrived. A companion quest that "finally delivers" it would be a fiction about a finished act. The errand is to read it, add nothing, and see that he is not waiting at his mark for an answer. Asking him, on that visit, who quieted the grove, is the question the mid-line refuses.

He will walk one gate after the fold quest is turned in as a refusal. The Held Curse's circle is not his arena. If the player takes the offer, he leaves before the circle arms. A shard set in gold is the offer accepted in another metal.

Maeris laughs, and the bog is the subject that stops the laugh. Greenmire is gone from the maps. The lattice shows through the underbrush, which is why the eight steps include a look at the other side and a return. Three of the steps are hers. Naming the invented one is the proof the walker came back. A ninth step is Lyra's refusal to teach, borrowed here only as a limit: the second-hour verb on Maeris's row is a stop, not a promotion of Maeris into Lyra's robe. The song from three centuries before the Thorns stays unsung. The basilisk stays on the overlook.

## The uncounted, the courier, the open table, the fourth that does not start

Nyx organized a resistance out of the people her old employer had already used. The trials on employees' children are the reason she left. Three of those children are adults now, and they do not know, from her, what was done to them. A follow slot that announces this in banter would be the whistle blown at the wrong door. What she will say is smaller and harder: the stray is a dog, the scar is skin, the missing-number token stays dark. Ink on that token ends the walk. Her cease-fire with Zero is unspoken and remains unspoken. The two hundred operatives do not pack into the player's shadow. One knuckle of light, a cable, a slate with names scratched out: that is the silhouette at a hundred metres.

Kel Sandren is the Hub's courier, twenty-two, Taro's cousin, bored by fighting, proud of an empty loss column. The volume row called his home Frontier because Silas's chain begins there. The bed is the courier house. The off-schedule portal is the secret he has not assigned to a listener. Finding him is the existing quest. After that he will teach the only rule this slice adds to the road: one walker, because he already pays a toll for himself and he will not be a crowd. He wants the sound of the gate. The theory, the Handshake, the parcel with no addressee but V., stay in the Frontier's locked lines. There is no seated clip, so a remembrance goes in the satchel or it does not go.

Esha Varan built an open table designed around every way the last ones died. She is not angry. The ledger works and it is small, and fame would kill it faster than the Tessera. The enforcer coat on her role id is a missing plate, not a membership. One brand on a hound is a name held. A second brand is the machine eating. If the furnace would see the seed library she turns around, token or no token. She is not brought to the Court to make Old Seam cry over a reunion the correspondence has already replaced. The ark stays a shadow on the sheet.

Calla Bren buried seventy-three after the third uprising and is leading the fourth on a quest this follow mode is forbidden to start. Hearing the plan is not dawn. The wraith stays unfinished because she has buried enough completed people. The mask is a question, and wearing it answers wrong. Zaen's two disguised meetings, and the girlhood tavern with Iyatte, are weapons if said aloud. She does not say them. Unburial, as her verb, opens a staple. It does not conquer the catalogue and it does not pull a fallen city back into a happy ending. She is not given a plaza mark. If a binder ever stands her inside the 42 m disk, the sword is a flower until she leaves.

## When they are quiet

A companion who is asleep, at a vigil, or locking a door does not deliver a line because the player walked up. Brackish and Old Seam are silent at Dominus and Solnus. Kel is silent in the dormitory and speakable at Solnus only because that hour is the log, not the bed. Maeris receives people at Freeus and Penanus and does not break the moon-vigil. Calla's Dominus hour is a vigil in which she speaks to no one. Esha's night is the letter to Seam and the lock on the ledger. The player is not invited in to either.

Nyx's thirty seconds before a hard call are a line that tells the player not to help. They are not a missing subtitle.

## What the errands are for

The name at the portal plaza is the rule, spoken by the person who crosses for a living. Naming none is a legal completion. The cast is not a debt.

The milepost is a child borrowing a true name and returning it to the tower. Seam walks because she already knows the letters. Nesha is not on the road.

The page Maren writes is the politics: two powers share a plaza every day and are not thereby allies. The slice refuses to launder a war crime into a buddy quest.

The frame on Nyx's wall is the other cross-world fact that was already done. Wolves and walls. Finished. The player is allowed to be late.

The minute at Penanus is Kel's own check, already on his schedule. The player watches, does not cross, and does not choose the listener he has not chosen. The courier sheet calls that slip the third clue toward a creature breach. Catching the creature would spend a clue this facet does not own. The log stays his page.

The braid is the other refusal. The beads are anchors, written as a weakness, not as loot. Counting them with him is the errand. Pocketing one sends him back to the mark the way the curse offer does. The guest is still standing there afterward.

No errand retunes a tariff, a boss clock, a density cap, or a spoke's own side quests. The creatures those bosses wear stay the creatures they were. Maeris still will not teach a crossing in exchange for a lattice fragment. The eight steps that already return are the crossing. The companions explain themselves by refusing the wrong attendance, which is a story, and by not arriving with a new monster to justify the refusal.
