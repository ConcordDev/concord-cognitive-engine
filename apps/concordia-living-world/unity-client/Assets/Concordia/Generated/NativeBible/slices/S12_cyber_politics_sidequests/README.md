## Scale

`countries.json` gives seven claim centers. Radii are 25.5 m except the Polysteel Society, the AI Rights Movement, and the Cordon Guild at 23 m. The circles overlap. They are claims, not floors you can walk as walls. District pins for the uniques are the runtime hash in `WorldGeography.BuildPlaces`, radius 8 m plus 2 m times the district index. S11's deck, hinge, and gap used the placed trade-zone anchors. Those floors are not moved. Schedule rooms that are not paired to an anchor in this slice spawn nobody.
