# Stories — S19 Sere

## The law that never became a gate

Sere is eight centuries of extraction on an Earth-shaped world, told as a machine nobody chairs. The First Twin Siege chartered the Tally House "until conditions stabilize," and the charter is still temporary because stability would end it. The Second Twin Siege raised the Clearing Spire with no flag, so there would be nothing to burn. The Long Mirror sold parity as a product: each breakthrough matched within a season, because a finished war ends the order book. House Aldermere cleared both sides and kept the fulcrum. The crest is a balance with no pans.

Three peoples tried to build outside that shape. The Almsfolk were lent to. The Lamplighters were ridiculed into silence. The Table-of-All was split by a border quarrel. None of them lost a battle that a Refusal could have named. The WorldDef states it in one line: no Refusal ever held here. The No under that line is the record of those three endings. The Mark is what compounded instead. Live steel is legal. Flower Law is the Court disk of 42 m, and a fight dragged back through that disk becomes flowers. It does not become Sere law.

The Ark Exodus is the bridge, not a ninth spoke. When the open ledger could not be allowed to root, the unbound built it above the atmosphere. Seven arks left the First Launch Cradle. You leave because you cannot refuse the machine from inside it. Tunya is the other end of the umbilical. The Crucible already carries the Eighth Refusal, the one that refuses to close, and that world's own facet keeps the sentence that there is no ninth. Sere does not complete the set by standing on the Hub ring.

So the door is a waystone. `Canon.Gates` lists eight worlds and stops. `MegaworldMap` puts Sere off the ring: Crime's angle, 5π/4, plus 0.35 rad, still 0.435 rad short of the Permanent Dawn, and out at 1.35 times the 220 m present-ring. The continent's point on that plane is (−125.27, −269.29). `HasLinkGate` is false. On the gate component itself, a destination of Sere zeroes the tariff, zeroes inspection, and leaves the owner blank. A spoke gate charges, inspects, and belongs to someone. A waystone is a step.

What the player is not told on a board: whether the Quiet Fall was a munition or a non-native arrival, the names Wend can match to half a tile, Lysandra's second ledger, the thought Tomas will not finish, the Archivist's mis-files, Sarn's paired manifests, and the longer list of people the Cradle refused a berth. Those stay in the canon files.
