# Stories — eighteen people who already stand

The Unburned Court is the battlefield left unpaved. Concord built the Link to watch what Concordia made. The Sovereign refused to close the eight gates, because he had already refused the idea of alone. Flower Law holds to 42 m. The guests stand inside it. The pillars stand on the unpaved ring: Concordia at the warm apex, Concord facing her, the Sovereign with his back to both.

S02 walked the politics those stands can actually hold, and said the rest of these lives did not need a second tutorial. This anthology is the remainder. Each person has a fact the lore or a spoke sheet already printed. Each hook asks the player to leave that fact unfinished.

The playable city is the Canon court. When a sheet describes a country, a mainframe, or a dynasty, the country stays on the far side of the gate. The body in the plaza is CX, at the GuestDef height, with the plate's one prop.

## The Lamplighter

Temple records hint that the lineage was once two people sharing one lantern and one name. He gives one true sentence to a newcomer, once, and is not seen by them again.
