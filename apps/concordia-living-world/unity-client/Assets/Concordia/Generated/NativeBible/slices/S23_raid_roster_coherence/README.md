## The law, short

A row qualifies only on the clock its own source already published. At one hub hour, one circle. A seat outranks a local. Among seats, `day_index % 9` rotates who stands first. A row arms only if it wins every hour of its own window. A partial claim yields the whole occurrence. A winner whose floor is still unplaced spawns nothing, and the yielded name does not step in.

Remembrance drops only from an armed occurrence whose phase law was kept, and it does not open another world's phase.
