# Stories — S05 Tunya

## The law the animals live under

Tunya's no is harvest. Fruit that has fallen may be carried. The trunk, the graft, and the root may not. While a person is not striking, the grove gives poise back. A strike spends that. Steel still cuts here. The flower disk of 42 m is the Hub's law, and a hunt that drags a jackal back through the Court to make the blade bloom has already left Tunya's argument.

The planet reached this argument before the arks. Ancient Tunyans had stone, brick, the song of trees, and the refusal-field. Seven arks came later, scattered by a wormhole that did not keep one clock. Africa's ark made the first city, on the cliffs that became Masond. The North American ark's Native passengers walked east and named themselves Corre. The rest founded Asbir in the southern dunes, beside an archive someone had curated before launch. Eurasia split into Bahiij, Dormas, Fluxom, and the interior walk that became Dinye. South America's ark came out of the fold late and hurt, and founded Aekon. A cohort off the African ark walked to the rivers and founded Sahm rather than disappear into Masond. The Vessine crashed in Year 11 on an island off the Vrellan coast and heal for food. Their goddess is Mercury. The Sanguire, a Vrellan people out of Sandrun, carry fire by bloodline.

None of that is a bestiary. It is why the bestiary is small. The playable animals of this facet belong to the Verdant Veil: terraces of timber and solar tile, a shelf under Nil's closed grove, a canopy that still argues about theft. The kingdoms are real in lore and in `countries.json` centers. They are not paddocks for these eight rows. Nil's inner grove is a compound refusal. People who cross it do not come back in the country text. No sealie, hound, goat, hare, heron, finch, harpy, jackal, wyrm, or stag is placed inside it. Aekon's twelfth district stays shut. The reap window stands on the lip, outside.

Electricity does not hold. Wires conduct and generators turn, and a completed circuit refuses. Powers and direct sun are what the planet allows. Ark panels wake on stored light for a moment and go dark. That fact is the heron's errand and the wyrm's opposite. It is not a license to wire the terrace.

The twelfth lip is a sentence in the volume encounter: the edge under the canopy. Aekon Glacier Gate at (−15, 68) is the ice gate, not that lip. Fluxom Gate at (62, 18) is a dye territory's door. Masond, Corre, Dormas, and the Vrellan state have factions and no country row, so they have no center this facet can honestly occupy. The Hold of First Arrival remains the pilgrimage `q_tunya_heron_hold` already extends. This slice does not add a bird inside it.

## Nil sealie

On the shelf it is a coastal mammal the size of a large dog lying down, a little shorter than a person is long. Paddle forelimbs, a whiskered face that reads as a dog, hind flippers as one paddle. Wet hide, sand on the belly, barnacle on one shoulder. It fishes, basks, and barks. Pairs and small groups, four alive at once, never a fleet.

The grove does not eat it and does not yoke it. "Take fruit, not the tree" covers animals that are not stock. A whisker on the bible loot list is not an errand.

Fluxom tells a different animal, and the content bestiary agrees with Fluxom. `content/world/tunya/bestiary.json` has a row `sealies`: tunyan native, bigger than a killer orca, razor teeth, a narwhal horn, speed that crosses miles in minutes. After a storm in which the two ruling families put their own people over the side, those animals came to the bodies and stayed for the ships. The file lists blubber, ivory that does not float, bone, and a purple defensive sac, and it calls a hunt a tier-1 event that loses ships. Whether they meant the dump is left open there. `Canon/tunya/` does not contain that file. The native bible does not contain that body. This facet does not close the open question by building the horn, and it does not pay ivory, blubber, or the sac. The merchant's errand is the smaller truth: walk the stair, see the loaf or see nothing, and do not sell a hunt. The dump itself is not dialogue. A people putting refugees in the water does not become a fishing minigame.

The two animals share a word. They do not share a rock. The shelf loaf is the only sealie with a mesh. The open-sea apex stays a sentence.

A flamingo mesh fails the shelf. The compiler may still offer one. The bind notes refuse it.

## Veil hound

Labor's dog. Square head, deep chest, sun-faded coat, a braided reed collar with one yellow thread. It heels, it bays, it nips a jackal that has started on a root. Two at a time, four on the whole terrace, denned at the upper stall after dusk.

The animal file marks the hound hostile. The work does not. In this instance it ignores a pilgrim. It answers a strike, and it answers a reap already in progress if it was called onto that root. Killing it for the nip is how a terrace loses its goats to the weed and then to the graft.

It is the same hound family as the coast and the marked dogs of other worlds, and it is not those dogs. No wolf coat, no fox stand-in, no person in a dog mask.

## Terrace goat

Block body, comma horns, a wooden bell, clay to the knee. Eight at a time on the strips between solar tile and vine. That diet is the farm. Clear tiles keep what little sun-storage the planet permits. Clear vines keep the graft, which is a living join and the nearest thing the terrace has to an ark: old wood taught to hold new wood.

They climb the 0.5 m steps. They headbutt if crowded on a stair. They are not ridden. The lead-rope errand already in the volume list is the lesson about the missing ride clip. This slice's goat errand is the other mistake, the one where a slipped lead puts three of them on a scar. Put them back on the weed. Leave the hide on the animal.

Dormas buys whole trunks with other kingdoms' money. The terrace's answer is the goat's diet and the stag's charge. This quest does not travel to Dormas to say so.

## Pollen hare

A pebble with ears. In bloom weeks the tips crust yellow, uneven, a coat of pollen rather than a light. Six of them, days, on the terrace floor and the canopy edge. Off bloom the cap is zero, so nobody farms a magic rabbit in the dry weeks.

They freeze, then bolt. Their place is prey and a trail. The ward on a reed pole is brushed from what they shook off, which the pollen quest already teaches. Pulling gold off the hare is a small reap. This facet does not add a second hare quest to repeat it.

## Ark heron

Tall when the neck is up, a gray stone when the neck folds. Algae on the legs, a nick in the bill, a clean chest. Three of them, day, in the irrigation cuts, spearing fish and frogs. Night they are gone from the budget.

Pilgrims come to Tunya to wake dead voices in ark terminals. A still heron looks like the same kind of object: a held shape, a memory you could catalogue. It is a bird having a successful hunt by not moving. The Hold of First Arrival already has a quest that sends you in as a guest and back out without a feather. This slice leaves that hold empty of extra herons and puts the living birds on the cuts, where the water is.

The photonic errand is one cracked tile in the sun, one second of stored light, the heron holding still because the glare is a good fishing light, and then dark. You do not complete a circuit. You do not mint the second as a recording. The Arks of Memory chain already does the terminal, the panels, and the DTU, with Torrek of Masond. A basket merchant on the lip is not that pilgrimage.

## Veil finch

A seed with a rust cap. Ten of them by day, pollen dusted, hopping the lips and the canopy edge. They move weed seed and grain along the terrace. A farm that loses them silts its tiles and then blames the goats. They are not loot and not adds.

The same species sits on the Sundering in pine dust, cap twelve, owned by the Fantasy creature slice. Tunya does not borrow that flock and does not repaint it.

## Veil harpy

Person-sized, a little over, torso like a person, arms as wings, legs as a bird and thick enough to read at a hundred meters. Short bill, pollen on the chest, a scrap of grove cloth on one hip, talons chipped. Four in the canopy between thefts, and none when the twelfth window is using them.

Their place in the law is the difference between a fine and a crime. Fruit and a dropped tool are theft. The merchant's existing errand is to pick the tool up and not hook the thief. The fine is fruit. A trunk is a reap, and that is the morning the alpha is dressed out of this same mesh. There is no second harpy species for the boss of that morning.

They scream in two notes. The second note is the dive. A carried tool on the hip cloth is a stagger if it is knocked loose. A broken wing wrist cannot grab. Both tells are already on the monster row. This slice does not add a soul-steal, a curse, or a costume.

Not a parrot. Not a CX body with cloth wings. Not pinup armor.

## Reap jackal

Narrow wedge, ears like dishes, tail a rod when it flanks. Red dust, sap on the muzzle, no armor. Five on a wounded edge, where a graft is already worried. Zero on the weed strips, zero in Nil, zero inside the twelfth.

They are what the Refusal is for. The veil does not call them game, does not pay a fang bounty, and does not send them into the inner grove as set dressing. A yip starts the flank. The wet crack is the root coming up. A hit on the ear breaks that telegraph for a few seconds. The hound's job is to be there when the yip starts, with a person who is not swinging at the tree.

The Superhero world's dawn jackal uses this body plan and adds a marble brow. It stays on that world.

## The Twelfth Reaper

Every third morning the grove board says a reap is scheduled, and that theft is not the crime. One harpy, the same mesh, is the alpha above the lip. Four jackals take the terrace. Wood bell, then the wrist, then the yip if you are late.

Phase one they steal tools and fruit. You can pick the goods up. Killing them here is not the fine. Phase two the alpha stops pretending and goes for a trunk. Open hands in the do-not-reap stance stagger that pass. Phase three the pack breaks, and the payment is fruit. A pinion on a belt is a story you failed even if the health bar says otherwise. The talon remembrance stays the volume's item.

The memory under the lip is the South American ark. The fold held those passengers until the ship limped here and Aekon was founded. The district called the twelfth has been closed since the kingdom's first year. The board on this window does not say why, and does not invite a step across the stone. The bloc quest already on the volume list is the walk to the boundary and the stop. This fight is the canopy's version of the same refusal: do not take the living thing to get at what is shut behind it.

If the lip has no transform, the morning emits nothing. The glacier gate is not a substitute.

## The bloom that takes the hour

Past the last terrace, in deep shade that is still outside Nil, one pollenwyrm keeps noon every second day. Basilisk coil, a mane of fat pollen bulbs, no wings. Bees, then the rasp, then a hood, then a short cloud. The cloud is an effect. It is not a second creature.

Burst one sac on the hood and the cloud is smaller. That is the weak point already written. Harvesting the mane, standing in the sleep to keep a dream, or killing the coil are the same failure: you reaped the hour. Leaving on the bee tell is the refusal. A person holding the grove stance, empty hands, is not striking, and the wyrm does not owe them a fight.

Ark terminals spend stored sun because a closed circuit dies. The wyrm spends attention. Sleep through the bloom and the season is gone with every trunk still standing. Pollen is the year's archive on a body. It is not a vial quest. The reed ward already has its own errand, brushed off a hare's trail.

The animal file does not mark the wyrm hostile. This window does, after the rasp, if you are still in the circle with a weapon or if you cut a graft to get there. Other hours, the cap is zero. It does not patrol the hall.

## The graft ward

Every fourth afternoon, if the twelfth morning is not already running, one veil stag walks the terrace lip. Stag rig shared with the pinewood herd, pollen instead of salt, two thick talons where the front hooves would be, hind hooves left so the gait stays a stag's. No wing membranes. A griffin silhouette means the bind is wrong and the animal should not be there.

It bugles at a bitten graft. A pilgrim with fallen fruit gets the bugle and not the charge. A saw, a blade, or a jackal on the scar gets the charge, and the hit plants a pollen ward, uneven, the same yellow as a honest reed. Break a fore talon and the charge shortens. Take the antler, or take the broken talon home, and the story fails. The Hub herd at Pinewood Crossing is a different slice's cap. This stag does not cross to the salt road to become a sixth deer, and it does not cross to the Sundering because the hybrid row also lists that world.

The memory is a graft. Old wood holding new wood is the ark lesson the terrace can teach without opening Asbir's vault. What was deleted before launch, and who on the survey ship knew the clocks would slip, is not on this board.

## Why there is no boss behind them

A boss in this bible is a personality and a law, on a body that has a `mon_boss_` id. Tunya's monster line is the harpy and the jackal. The twelfth event already gives those bodies a morning, a fine, and a law. The wyrm and the stag are one each, on clocks that do not stack with that morning, and each one can be refused without a region-clear tag. Scaling either of them up, or borrowing the Held Curse, the Unfinished, the Census, the Un-Ender, or the Compound Mark, would put another world's law on this grove.

People fight in Capoeira here. This facet spawns no person to do it. Sanguire fire, Vessine healing, and the child both houses have agreed not to name stay on their lore entries. They are not creature powers.

## Weed, not the graft

A laborer on the lower terrace, one of the six the density already budgets, lost a lead at midday. Three goats did what goats do. They left the weed, which is their work, and started on a graft scar, which is the trunk in smaller form. Dormas will pay for a whole trunk. The terrace will not sell one to fix a noon mistake.

You lead them back between the tiles and the vine. The bell stays wood. Nobody sits on a back. If the terrace set is not placed, you wait, and you do not march the corn props into a quest. Hide is not the wage.

This is not the volume's lead-one-switchback lesson, and it is not the fallen-fruit sale.

## The horn is a story

A merchant at the baskets, one of three, has been repeating a Fluxom line: the shelf beast is a horned killer the size of a ship, and a hunt would settle an old water debt. The merchant has not been to the stair.

You go to the outer stair only. The threshold stays shut. What you are allowed to see is the bible animal, low and dog-faced, or an honest gap where the mesh should be. A flamingo, a horn, or a body longer than you fails the report. You do not strike it, and you do not bring the merchant a whisker.

You also do not explain the dump. The families, the two hundred, the word traitor, the open question of whether the animals agreed: all of that can stay in the lore entry. The quest is the size of the lie a market can tell when the coast is a long walk.

The volume already has the not-a-flamingo walk, extended from Nil's silent-walk protection. This errand is the other false body. Passing one does not complete the other.

## The hound keeps the root

On the wounded edge, still the terrace set, jackals have found a root that a goat or a storm already loosened. The laborer there does not want a pelt. The hound knows the yip. You heel one dog, you hold the stance that restores poise because you are not striking the tree, and you spoil the flank on the ear tell. The root stays down.

A dead hound, a taken fang, a cut trunk, or a step into the twelfth fails it. The twelfth morning is a different clock and a different lip. The reap-fine quest is a tool under a harpy, not this edge. Do-not-reap is already the default presence stance on Tunya. This errand does not pretend to teach it for the first time.

## One second of sun

At the irrigation lip a cracked solar tile does what Tunya allows and no more. Sun sits in it. For about a second it is awake, the way an ark panel is awake, photonic and not a circuit. A heron in the cut holds still. Then the tile is dark and the bird is a bird. Folded, it looks like stone. It is breathing.

You tell the merchant that, and you leave the feather and the panel. Wiring a second tile completes a circuit, and the planet refuses the current. The refusal is the lesson. Minting the second as a Web-DTU is the Masond pilgrimage, giver Torrek, three panels, the Africa ark core. A merchant on a lip does not get that chain, and does not get the curated vault, and does not hear what the deletion index holds.

## The flock stays on the tile

A laborer at the upper stall, still one of the six, has heard pilgrims say the finches belong to the ark because the panels and the tiles are the same trick. They are the same trick. They are not the same ground.

You watch the flock on the lips, rust caps, pollen dust, seed moving so the tile can see the sun. Then you walk the exterior sand at the Asbir Ark, the pin at (−90, −58). The door stays as you found it. Within forty meters the sand holds none of this bestiary: no finch, no heron pretending the dunes are a cut, no goat hired to graze a hull. If the terrace set is not placed, you wait on the first step. If the finch mesh is missing, an empty lip and an empty dune are both honest, and a bird that appears on the hull fails the errand.

You turn in on the terrace. The wright at the terminal is someone else's walk, the one that leaves the anomaly dark. Torrek's three panels are someone else's harvest. The deletion index is not a thing this laborer is selling.

The heron is still the wading bird of the seven-ark memory, and it is still in the irrigation cut. The sealie is still older than the hulls, and it is still the loaf on the shelf. Memory named them. Water and weed keep them.

## One terrace through a day

Goats clear the weed so a solar tile can take the only light the planet stores. Finches move the seed that would silt that tile. In a bloom week hares shake pollen onto the floor, and labor brushes the floor, not the hare. Herons stand in the cuts and spear fish. Hounds keep goats on the weed and answer a jackal that has started on a root. Harpies visit for fruit and tools. Jackals stay on the wounded edge.

Every third morning that visit is cancelled. The same harpy and the same jackals are the twelfth window, on the lip, and the terrace counts of both drop to zero so the pack is not doubled. At noon on the off-mornings, one pollenwyrm past the last terrace offers sleep. Every fourth afternoon, if that morning is not already running, one stag walks a bitten graft and plants pollen instead of collecting an antler. At night the hounds den at the upper stall. The rest of this list is at cap zero. The shoal sealie never joins the terrace count.

Bahiij's elephants, Asbir's shellfish paper, Fluxom's dye-bugs, and the horned sealie are in the content bestiary or the country text. They are not on this clock. A goat from the content herd list is this terrace goat or it is nothing. Oxen, rams, and mountain goats do not get a second mesh.

## What the animals do not say

The survey crew knew the dilation and left it out of the briefing. Corre's elders walked because they knew what was deleted. The twelfth's dead are not all dead, and the Bloc holds because two other kingdoms know. The Vessine home coordinates pass at an Elder's death and have not been sailed. Dormas's upper house is a Vrellan exile price. A Sanguire-Vessine child exists and both houses are silent. The Concord Link sometimes speaks through a woken terminal, and the Vessine can name that substrate and have not.

Those are lore truths. They are not barks, not board lines, and not turn-in text for these five errands. Yenna of Nil is not a giver here. The inner grove is not a visit. The wright is not a giver here.
