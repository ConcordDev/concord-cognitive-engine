# S13 — Frontier salt, drift, mounts, and the road

Frontier only. Timber, salvage plate, canvas, open sky. The salt lens is the overlap of two country discs. The drift gap is the wind between two other rims. Horses are led. Pronghorn are called and followed. The road's people are CX. The dome stays down.
