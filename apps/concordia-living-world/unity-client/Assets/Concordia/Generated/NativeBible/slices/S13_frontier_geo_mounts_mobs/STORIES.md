# Stories — the road that will not close

## The law on this ground

The Frontier refuses the dome. The line on the gate is "The road is our door." The other half of that law is the one people learn with their shoulders: no dome means no shelter. Drop the covering. Wind does the rest. Steel is live here. A blade carried back inside the Hub disk of 42 m, off the Arena sand, is a flower again. The Hub-ring gate stone sits at (−34, 0), thirty-four meters from the plaza origin, inside that disk. The speech can be read there. The fight belongs further out, where the country discs actually are.

People on this road use Muay Thai because the world default is close and windy. The iron they carry is a spear-length wagon bar, `wpn_2h_frontier_wagoniron`. The role file still names `wpn_pole_frontier_wagoniron`, which is not a weapon row. Bind the bar that exists. The skinner is `wpn_1h_frontier_skinner`. A rifle stays in old sentences about Zara Morn and the pioneer list. Firearm coverage is empty, so those sentences do not become a mesh in anyone's hands.

Palette of the ground: `#c8b070`, `#efe6d4`, `#4a3828`, with rust `#8a6a48` where salvage plate has been hammered onto timber. Canvas is the one light mass. One emissive would be a camp lamp, and this facet does not require one. At 100 m a horse is a deep chest, a wolf is a lean chevron, a pronghorn herd is one pale flag, a domebreaker is a hump and a single horn bar, a dustgriffin is a griffin with rectangles of sail on the wings.
