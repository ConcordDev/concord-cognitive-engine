# S11 — The Grid, drones and the decks they count

Cyber only. A counted eye on a thick disc, one door-shouldered sentinel, one construct with the serial ground off, the organic remainder (a one-eared dog and a sparrow on fiber), and the eight-meter sky deck where the street census cannot write.
