# The Crucible — style board

**WorldId:** `Crucible`  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_crucible_lattice`  
**DressVocab:** no dedicated token. Do not borrow Frontier canvas as the foundation or Grid acrylic as the wall. Dark oak and a quartz collar on CX cloth is the wardrobe.  
**Fight style:** Sword, as the Canon default for this world.  
**Thesis:** Arcane and cosmic as physical materials: basalt, quartz, obsidian glass, dark oak. Patterns stay unclosed. Stairs lean and still snap.

Teal `#20ffd0` is a seam, not a coat of paint. Lilac `#c8b4ff` is a rare second glint and should not appear on the same hero as a full teal wash. Canon sun is the teal. Fog `#3a5558`.

## Material language

Basalt, iridescent quartz used as a rib, obsidian glass that is dark, dark oak, tool marks. Wear is a chip, grit, a seam that stops. No sparkle cloud. No floating castle. Steampunk instruments belong at the Unclosed Foundry blend, not as this hall's costume.

## Architecture silhouette

Lattice hall: two walls and a vault that does not meet. Quartz facade: one rib on a dark bay. Leaning stair: a tilted flight with level treads. Interrupted road: a slab that ends. The 100 m read is the gap in the vault and the lean of the stair. Drift creatures are not architecture.

## Flora silhouette

Seam lichen: a dull crust on the teal line. Fault moss: one pad. No glowing forest. The biome is rock and a few pads so the seam stays visible.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Unstable HDRI: two temperatures allowed in one sky, warm and teal, with the contradiction in the clouds and not in a UI overlay. Fog in the seams. Hero landmarks stay legible when the macro drifts. Canon weather: drift. Night: the seam is the practical.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX for people, including the lattice walker. Oak-dark cloth, one quartz clasp. The Un-Ender and the drift mass are not people and are not this wardrobe. No ninth-Refusal costume. There is no ninth.

## VFX color grammar

Un-end is a teal seam that opens again. Shard hits are physical chips, lilac for one frame at most. Recycle is the same object returning, not a particle nova. Completion is the picture you do not draw.

## Do

- Stop arches short.
- Keep treads level when the flight leans.
- Snap landings to 4 m.

## Don't

- Don't close the circle to make it pretty.
- Don't add a face to a basalt boulder.
- Don't sparkle the quartz into a gem halo.
- Don't put a Flower Law urn on the lattice.

## 100 m silhouette checklist

- Hall: a rectangle missing its top key.
- Stair: a tilted bar of steps.
- Seam rock: a lump with one bright crack.
- Lichen: a dull line on that crack, not the light itself.

## SoftEnter first frame

Basalt underfoot, a stair that leans, the vault overhead stopping short, one teal crack, lilac absent unless a shard is in the air. The road behind you is already interrupted. Steel is live. If it would end, it does not.
