# Tunya — style board

**WorldId:** `Tunya`  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_tunya_veil`  
**DressVocab fallback:** `grove`  
**Fight style:** Capoeira for people. Beasts do not get Capoeira.  
**Thesis:** The largest multi-biome ark continent, country by country: cliff, dune, forge, glacier, harbor, listening grove, sharing solar tile, limestone, and the law that fruit is taken and the tree remains.

## Material language

CLT timber, limestone, solar tile, dye-stained wet wood, blackened forge stone, ark-hull metal in ice, salt crust on dunes. Grafts are thick branches used as lintels. Water runs in terrace lips and canal locks. Wear is soil, pollen, sanitation crust, soot on one face of a stack.

## Architecture silhouette

Country first. Masond is a cliff stair. Asbir is a rectangle buried in a dune. Sangree is a square stack. Aekon is a curved rib with a warm door. Fluxom is a lockhouse on a canal. Nil is almost no building: a root. The shared hall is a terrace step, low, with one solar eave. Foundation stays grove timber even when Fluxom adds a brass pipe.

## Flora silhouette

Fruit tree: low wide crown, three fruits. Nil fig: buttress walls. Cliff reed: one vertical bundle. Dye reed: stained to a line. Ash scrub: a low soot dome. Ice-rim grass: a frost wedge, not a crystal flower. These six must not collapse into one jungle kit.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Six intents, one continent. Masond: hard river daylight, mist in the gorge. Asbir: high hard sun, short shadows, pale sky. Sangree: ash-warm HDRI, low contrast. Aekon: blue-white, a warm practical in the rib. Fluxom: overcast, wet. Nil: dark canopy, almost no sky, one lichen. Fog `#e4d7b0` is the shared dust-pollen veil on the terraces between them. Canon weather: grove.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Grove cloth, seed jar, dye on a cuff, ark quilt as a real patch. Capoeira reads in the stance, not in a costume silhouette that hides the body. No musket-staff. Firearm coverage is zero. No ice-blade hero.

## VFX color grammar

Pollen ward is warm `#f0d080`, a stance, few grains. Fruit picked is a small orange. Solar wake is a dull square of tile catching sun, not a circuit trace. Forge mouth is a small amber, contained. Glacier has no spell ice.
