# Permanent Dawn — style board

**WorldId:** `Superhero`  
**Display:** The Permanent Dawn. Lore also says The Superhero City. Visual bible sector: Aegis.  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_superhero_dawn`  
**DressVocab:** civic cloth. Do not fall back to `grid` acrylic or `street` tenement as the foundation.  
**Fight style:** Karate.  
**Thesis:** A modern metropolis of glass, steel, and pale composite where height is a verb and mercy refuses the last blow.

Ground in Canon is `#4a5870` (street shade) while the hero architecture is `#d8d4dc`. Both stay. Emissive is dawn gold `#ffd0a0` in windows and on a pad lip, not a laser. Accent blue `#3a78ff` is a small inlay.

## Material language

Marble composite, chrome that chips back to the composite, self-clean glass with one honest crack, concrete transit piers. Wear is a landing scuff, a washed ghost of paint, gasket dust. Parks and low-rent arterials are the same city. Neural clinics are canopies with a lamp, ordinary.

## Architecture silhouette

Kane fin: one vertical notch, the skyline piece. Roof pad: a circle and a lip. Spire hall: a tall pale bay with one glass slot. Arterial pier: a concrete Y. Mercy ring: a low circle with a walking gap. At 100 m the fin and the pad separate from a Grid bridge (the pad is a circle; the bridge is a bar).

## Flora silhouette

Park plane: high crown, one prune scar, cleaner than the Coast plane. Roof olive: low, gray-green, square planter. No rooftop jungle.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Permanent dawn HDRI: low warm sun that does not finish rising, long gold shadows, fog `#e7e4ea` in the avenues. Night exists as a deeper blue with the same warm windows; the sun never becomes a victory stamp. Canon weather: dawn.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Civic cuts, a circlet only if the prop is the mercy circlet already in the bible, Karate in the hands. Capes, if any, are cloth with weight, not a VFX sheet, and they are not required. No pinup armor. The Luminary is a law you refuse, not a final-form costume.

## VFX color grammar

Mercy shock is a gold ring that stops. Fist impact is physical, short, warm. Refuse-the-win is the gold failing to close. No killing beam. No aura that replaces the body.

## Do

- Chip the chrome.
- Keep a low-rent arterial in the same palette, dirtier concrete.
- Leave a gap in the mercy ring so a person can enter.

## Don't

- Don't build a crystal palace with no street.
- Don't take the sunrise as a win-state sky.
- Don't confuse the Dawn gate at Hub (0, -34) m with the Task Force capital's local 42 m. That nearness is not Flower Law.
- Don't reskin the Held Crown Stair as pure marble. Granite stays the foundation.

## 100 m silhouette checklist

- Fin: a notched blade standing up.
- Pad: a circle.
- Pier: a Y.
- Olive: a low gray ball in a square, distinct from the park plane.

## SoftEnter first frame

Gold air, a roof lip at eye level if you arrived high, otherwise the mercy ring and a park plane, glass carrying the unfinished sunrise, chrome already chipped. Steel is live. The dawn does not end.
