## Material language

Weathered limestone, court cobble, warm brass, timber lintels, cloth awnings. Wear is grit in joints, soot on lantern cowls, gall on brass, a petal stain. Albedo carries no baked sun. Bevels on every quoin and jamb catch the chase camera. Upper floors may take a cloth retrofit. They do not take Grid acrylic.

## Architecture silhouette

Low and legible. Halls are rectangles with a simple pitch. The Ring of Doors is a drum, not a triumphal arch repeated eight times. The Arena arch is thicker than a court door and frames sand. The wall at 56 m is a band, not a castle skyline. Traversal: lantern bracket at 2.2 m, quoin ledge, market rail, arena stair. Scaffold only outside the law radius.

## Flora silhouette

Court linden: one oval crown, clear stem. Pinewood pine: layered plates, salt on the windward bark. Box shrub: a cube with one bloom. Verge grass: one wedge. No tropical kit, no glowing grove.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Day: warm high key, crisp colored shadows, a pale gold HDRI with a soft cloud bank. Fog `#d9cbb8` sits beyond the wall so the Crown Roads fade before the 220 m Present ring. Night: lantern practicals, low fill, the plaza still readable. No neon night. Weather default in Canon is clear.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX body, Concordia face. Court cloth, a short mantle, brass pin, flower only as a real sprig or the urn's one bloom. Gale's Arena kit may show a rack. Wardens read heavier than archivists. Brackish reads smaller (1.42 m) and still CX. Quaternius is not a body.

## VFX color grammar

Flower quench is warm orange into petal, short, on the blade that dies inside 42 m. Lantern pulse is the same orange, dim. Archive dust is neutral. No cyan, no magenta, no furnace eye.
