## Material language

Weathered granite, moss, dark timber, gold leaf used once per hero. Water is a sheet on a shelf. Roots are bark, thick enough to grab. Wear is rain dark, lichen, harness gall on a toll chain. No shiny castle set.

## Architecture silhouette

Titan pillar is the skyline: one broken column. Halls sit low against it. Wildwood facades are stone at the foundation and timber above. Doors stay human (1.2 × 2.2). The Held shrine is a short hall, not a dragon gate. House Voss stairs are four treads with the gold gone.

## Flora silhouette

Thornwood oak: pillar trunk, one mantle limb. Fold willow: thick trunk, one whip curtain. Rune-scar pine: a pine, the scar pale and unlit. Fern: one stump fountain. These must separate from Hub pine and from Tunya's low fruit crown.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Day: green-gold canopy HDRI, god rays in plates, not volume soup. Fog `#b7c4b0` in the fold. Alpine rim is clearer and colder. Night: almost no practicals; the gold scar is the one light. Weather: clear, with waterfall mist as a local volume.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Sword at the hip outside the Hub. Ward cloth, a closed crest, Thornwood leather. Thorne's inward-folded blade is a prop state, not a new body. No generic orc kit, no plate bikini, no dragon-rider costume.

## VFX color grammar

Curse-fold is a gold line that closes. Ward-cut is a pale edge on steel. Pollen, if it appears, is a few grains, not a nova. Hostility paying poise is a brief inward gold, not an outward fire.

## Do

- Keep gold thin and worn.
- Make the pillar the 100 m read.
- Leave the basilisk's fold as a lip of stone when the creature is absent.

## Don't

- Don't build a shiny white castle.
- Don't mount the Held Curse as a dragon.
- Don't put Flower Law urns on the wild road.
- Don't glow the rune scar.

## 100 m silhouette checklist

- Pillar: a broken vertical, wider than a tree.
- Oak: a barbed lollipop with one horizontal limb.
- Shrine hall: a low dark rectangle beside the pillar.
- Willow: a thick trunk under a curtain, distinct from the oak.

## SoftEnter first frame

Green air, wet granite, the titan pillar off-center, a ward stone at the knee, gold only in one worn vein, the Crown Road behind you already browner than this moss. Steel stays steel.
