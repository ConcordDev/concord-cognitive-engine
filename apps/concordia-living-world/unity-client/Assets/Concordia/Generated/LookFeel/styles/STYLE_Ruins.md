# Sovereign Ruins — style board

**WorldId:** `Ruins`  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_ruins_ash`  
**DressVocab fallback:** `ash`  
**Fight style:** Sword.  
**Thesis:** A silent classical archive that is abandoned and still working: limestone, ash, fossil rib, cold iron, endings that do not finish.

Emissive is a dull page-amber `#c4a070`, rare. The dark accent `#2a1c14` is wet ash and iron, not a hell mouth. Canon sun `#c8a070` is dust-filtered.

## Material language

Limestone drums, cracked street, fossil bone used as structure, cold iron pins, moss, a real sheet of water from a spout. Wear is ash dust, mineral stain, a crack that stops mid-line. No skull decoration. No lava.

## Architecture silhouette

Forum column and the unfinished arch are the pair: one is stacked drums, the other is a gap where a keystone would be. Rib bridge is one bone curve. Catalogue hall is a colonnade with a dark interior. Human doors. The skyline is broken verticals of similar width, separated by the gap of the unfinished arch.

## Flora silhouette

Ash cypress: a narrow dark flame on a thick trunk. Catalogue vine: one rope. Bone moss: a pad on a curve. No jungle. Birds are a separate fauna pass.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Ash HDRI, sun a dull coin, fog `#8a8178` always a little present. Rain is allowed and makes the spout honest. Night: almost dark, one amber in a hall, the rest moonless dust. Canon weather: ash.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Ash-gray cloth, a slate, sword. Keepers read heavier. A spell-spirit, if it exists, is not a person and not this wardrobe. Masks only where the monster row already asks. No lich costume.

## VFX color grammar

Unburial is a lift of dust, neutral. Refuse-ending is a line that stops, amber, short. No green necromancy glow. No skull fire.

## Do

- Leave inscriptions unfinished.
- Keep water in the aqueduct real and thin.
- Use rib as structure with iron pins.

## Don't
