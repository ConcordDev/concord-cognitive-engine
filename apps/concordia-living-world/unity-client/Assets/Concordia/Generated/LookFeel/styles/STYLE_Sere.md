# Sere — style board

**WorldId:** `Sere`  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_sere_mark`  
**DressVocab:** smoked `street` cloth and a brand tile. Not `court`.  
**Fight style:** Wing Chun.  
**Thesis:** Earth after centuries of extraction. Old capital, spire, furnace, breadland, flood, passes, haven, delta, highland. Exhausted infrastructure. The Mark compounds. No Flower Law.

Furnace eye `#e07030` is the one emissive, small, in a mouth of iron. Accent `#8a3030` is brand and rust, not a lava biome. Canon sun `#c8a060` is smog-filtered. Fog `#6a5848`.

## Material language

Soot brick, oil concrete, brand tile, rusted steel, rebar, dented silo, tide stain, polluted water. Wear is the history: repair, flood line, extraction. Not a stylized wasteland costume of spikes and skulls. Not a clean factory ad.

## Architecture silhouette

Furnace stack: a brick tower with a small eye. Spire stub: a thick broken shaft. Headframe: an A and one wheel. Silo: a dented cylinder. Tally hall: a low brick bay. Waystone: a rough slab, no arch. These must not read as the Grid (no acrylic) or the Coast (sodium teardrop is absent; the eye is in the stack).

## Flora silhouette

Furnace scrub: low, black on one side. Flood reed: a stain line. Stubble: a gray mat. Highland thorn: a lean bush. No heroic lone tree of hope unless it is the thorn, and the thorn is not hopeful.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Smog HDRI, low contrast, a brown-gold sun, fog always in the midground. Flood sectors pick up a flat gray water HDRI. Highland is windier and slightly clearer, still dirty. Night: the furnace eye and almost nothing else. Canon weather: smog.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX for people. Wing Chun. Smoked cloth, a brand tile as a pin or a floor, not a glowing tattoo suit. The invoice watcher may be masked and still CX. Kiln, hound, roach, and rat are not people. No court flower. No Quaternius body.

## VFX color grammar

Broken tile is a red-brown chip. Clearing is a short absence of smog, local, honest. The furnace eye does not become a beam. The Mark does not sparkle. Name-the-holder is a word on paper or tile, not a floating nameplate baked into the world mesh.

## Do

- Show repair and flood on the same concrete.
- Keep the waystone arcless.
- Let regions differ: breadland stubble, flood pier, highland headframe.

## Don't

- Don't style Sere as a fun scrapyard.
- Don't add a ninth Link gate.
- Don't plant a Flower Law urn.
- Don't turn the brand into a magic circle.

## 100 m silhouette checklist

- Stack: tower with a small bright mouth.
- Headframe: an A and a wheel.
- Silo: a cylinder with a torn cone.
- Waystone: a slab, distinct from a Hub drum.
- Scrub: a low one-sided bush, not a tree.

## SoftEnter first frame

Smog, oil concrete, a brand tile at the foot, the furnace eye small and already lit, flood stain if you came by water, the waystone only if this is the strait. No plaque from the Hub is in this frame. Steel is live. No Refusal ever held here.
