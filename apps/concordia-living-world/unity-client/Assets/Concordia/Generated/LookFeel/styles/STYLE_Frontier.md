# The Frontier — style board

**WorldId:** `Frontier`  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_frontier_road`  
**DressVocab fallback:** `drift`  
**Fight style:** Muay Thai.  
**Thesis:** Low-magic open road. Canvas, dust, cairns, relay posts. Shelter is a lean-to. A dome is the thing this country refuses.

Sun `#ffe8b0` is the wide sky. Emissive is a small warm lamp on a relay, rare. Accent `#4a3828` is iron and wet wood. Canon ground `#c8b070`.

## Material language

Timber, salvage plate, canvas, dust, nail, a wind-scoured mast. Wear is sun bleach, grit in a seam, canvas patch. No armor-castle. Salvage that shows up at the Unclosed Foundry may be Frontier plate; on this continent it stays a cabin and a bridge.

## Architecture silhouette

Cabin: a low rectangle and a porch post. Canvas facade: a laced panel. Relay: a mast with a box. Cairn: three stones and a stick. Wagon step: three treads, no wagon required in the same mesh. The road-is-door post is an open ring with no leaf. These read as small against the sky. The 100 m test is the mast and the cottonwood, not a fort.

## Flora silhouette

Wind grass: a bent comb. Dust sage: a silver dome. Cottonwood: a pale mass on a thick trunk, the water tree. No pinewood pine here. No mesa is placed; do not require one.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Huge HDRI, high sun, long shadows, dust veils `#e6d8b8` near the ground. Wind is the weather. Night: stars, one relay lamp, no city glow. Canon weather: wind.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Canvas, dust, a lead rope. Muay Thai in the stance. Horses are fauna, not a costume. No rider in a saddle until a ride controller exists. The Last Dome encounter stays unplaced: one mile past the gate is a sentence, not a coordinate this board invents.

## VFX color grammar

Dust-kick is a low tan fan. Dust sprint is the same tan, horizontal. Leaving a dome is an absence: do not draw a shield bubble to show it breaking. Canvas snap is cloth, not a metal iris.

## Do

- Keep the horizon empty often.
- Build bridges by hand, two logs and a deck.
- Point cairns with a stick.

## Don't

- Don't roof the road with a dome.
- Don't invent the Last Dome's mile in metres.
- Don't plant a Hub pine as the hero tree.
- Don't stake a sail into a permanent roof.

## 100 m silhouette checklist

- Mast: a thin vertical with one box, guy lines thick.
- Cottonwood: pale mass, distinct from sage.
- Cabin: a low bar with a post.
- Cairn: three lumps and a stick, distinct from a person.

## SoftEnter first frame

Open sky, a cairn, the road as two ruts, a relay if you are near the heart, dust at the ankle, no wall behind you except the memory of the Hub. Steel is live. The road is the door.
