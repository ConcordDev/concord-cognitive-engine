Sodium `#e0b050` is the only night emissive. Accent `#c8a060` is wet timber and paper, not gold leaf. Canon sun `#887060` is a rainy day.

## Material language

Stained brick, rusted corrugate, asphalt, rebar, cast iron, concrete piers, steam. Wear is peel, drip stain, rope groove, a milk crate. Water beads. Neon tubes do not belong on this kit. At the Uncounted Wharf the pipe may turn cyan at one end; the floor stays Coast asphalt.

## Architecture silhouette

Tenement: a tall brick rectangle with a Z of escape. Warehouse: a wide corrugate mouth. Overpass pier: a stained Y. Wharf: a bollard mushroom. These four separate at 100 m. Fire escapes are thick iron. Doors clear 2.2 m when open.

## Flora silhouette

Plane tree: mottled, high crown, clear stem. Gutter fern: one crack. Dock reed: one oil-dark wedge. No palms as a costume. No hedge-maze estate.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Overcast rain HDRI, wet reflections, fog `#6a6258` down the avenues. Night: sodium pools, headlights as small warm cones, interiors tungsten. No magenta sky. Canon weather: rain.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Street layers, a wet collar, Wing Chun in the stance. Syndicate weight reads in a heavier coat, still the same body. No rifle hero. Shotgun props stay off this mesh pass while firearm verbs are at zero. Witnesses are people, not icons.

## VFX color grammar

The bill is a paper flash, warm, then gone. Invoice steel is a short sodium spark on a blade edge. Rain is the ambient. No cyan pulse. Delay does not get a "cancelled" green.

## Do

- Let sodium own the night.
- Keep brick as the foundation under any wharf retrofit.
- Post paper on timber, not on a hologram.

## Don't

- Don't turn the Coast into cyberpunk.
- Don't add a neon ad sheet to a tenement.
- Don't put Flower Law urns on the wharf.
- Don't build a firearm hero mesh.
