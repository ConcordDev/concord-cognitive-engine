# The Grid — style board

**WorldId:** `Cyber`  
**Display:** The Grid  
**Kit:** `arch_kit_cyber_grid`  
**DressVocab fallback:** `grid`  
**Fight style:** Capoeira.  
**Thesis:** A near-future megacity of carbon, scratched glass, and physical neon, where identity is terrain and one number is missing.

Cyan `#3dffa0` is the single emissive family. Magenta `#c45aa8` is a reflection and a banner hem, not a second sun. Canon sun is that magenta as a sky bias, kept dimmer than the practical.

## Material language

Carbon, gunmetal, scratched acrylic, wet pavement, cable, service paint worn to primer. Neon is a glass tube on cloth. Hologram clothing is out. Glitch is a mis-seated physical sheet. Wear is soot, gasket moss, a ground-off serial that is a scratch, not readable type.

## Architecture silhouette

Sky-bridge: a bar. Census plinth: a low drum with a gapped ring. Service corridor: a pipe you can grab. Hatch: a square in the street. Tower fins exist as masses behind, but the hero kit piece is the bridge segment so the chase camera has a grab. Under-District is low. Stacks are the L0 skyline.

## Flora silhouette

Almost none. A weed in a well. A moss pad on a gasket. If a block reads as a park, it has failed this world. The plane tree belongs to the Coast and to Dawn.

## Sky, fog, HDRI

Night-biased HDRI even at noon: haze `#2a2040`, wet streets, a cyan practical every block and only one in the hero frame. Magenta sky leak at the horizon. Canon weather: neon. Rain is allowed and must not become the Coast's sodium picture; the lamp head here is a tube, not a teardrop.

## Wardrobe and culture kit

CX. Grid cloth, a slate, a collar. Capoeira reads on the body. Census officers may wear a mask; the face under it is still the Concordia face when shown. Drones are not people and are not this kit. No hologram jacket.

## VFX color grammar

Pulse is a cyan tick. Number-break is the missing digit going dark, not an explosion. Null flush is a short horizontal wipe of `#3dffa0`. Damage that is hidden is a UI law, not an invisibility shader on the street.

## Do

- Bolt every light to a surface.
- Leave one numeral missing on the plinth.
- Keep service pipes thick.

## Don't

- Don't float UI in the air as the architecture.
- Don't give the drone propellers. That failure mode is already documented on the creature pass.
- Don't sodium-wash a Grid alley.
- Don't count the Uncounted Wharf cleanly. The joint is allowed to be illegal and physical.

## 100 m silhouette checklist

- Bridge: a horizontal bar.
- Banner: a hanging rectangle with one bright hem.
- Plinth: a low drum.
- Weed: a small clump, not a tree.

## SoftEnter first frame

Wet dark pavement, one cyan tube on a soot banner, the census plinth with its gap, a hatch raised a hand, haze instead of a sunset. Steel is live. You are a body the stacks would like to number, and the plaque you came through is already behind you.
