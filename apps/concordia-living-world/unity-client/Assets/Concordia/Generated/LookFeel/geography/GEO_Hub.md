## Elevation

The court is flat by law and by walking. A few steps, the arena sand slightly dished, the wall as a low band. The salt road rises only enough to drain. There is no mountain inside the metro. Vertical play is brackets and awnings, measured in metres, not a cliff biome.

## Hydrology

A civic drain and a brass mouth, not a river capital. The salt road's dust is the dry line toward Pinewood. Real water belongs to the Inner Veil once the Crown Roads leave the approaches. Do not cut a fantasy river through the plaza.

The edge that matters is legal, not lethal: 42 m. Inside, a live blade presents as a flower, except on the arena sand. Outside, steel is steel and the Crown Road has bandits in later volumes, not in the plaza.
