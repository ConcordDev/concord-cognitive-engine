# Geography — Crucible

**WorldId:** `Crucible`  
**Center:** (+282.84 km, −282.84 km), southeast, gate 7π/4. Disc 110 km, design-intent.  
**Climate:** Drift. Canon weather is drift. Two temperatures may share a sky. The ground under a hero landmark stays readable.

## Elevation

Folded basalt, a fault that steps, a road that ends. Macro geography is allowed to contradict itself: a slab higher than the road that fed it, water that runs toward a seam and stops. Hero stairs still land on the 4 m grid. The lean is the landmark. The snap is the contract.

## Hydrology

Contradictory water. A sheet that should fall and does not, or a fault with moss and no stream. Do not build a pretty river city. The Unclosed Foundry, at the disc's edge toward Coast and Frontier scrap, has industrial steam and a vent. That steam is the blend, not the whole continent.

## Settlement

The open lattice at the heart. The foundry edge as a sector toward the blend. People are few relative to the Grid. The lattice walker is a person. Drift masses are not populations. There is no ninth district and no closed capital.

## Hazards and edges

North march meets Ruins. West march meets the Dawn. Northeast is the weak seam toward the Grid, walked slowly because a finished road would look like completion. Crown Road runs northwest to the Hub.

The hazard is instability of presentation, bounded so L3 landmarks stay legible. Do not delete collision with a drift shader.

## Law on the ground

Steel is live. If a pattern would end, the arch stops short. Flower Law does not apply.
