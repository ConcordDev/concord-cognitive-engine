# Geography — Sere

**WorldId:** `Sere`  
**Center:** (−227.76 km, −489.62 km). Radius factor 1.35 on the ring, Crime's angle plus 0.35 rad. Disc 80 km, design-intent.  
**Climate:** Smog, extraction, flood. Canon weather is smog. No Flower Law. No Link.

Sere is the wound on the far side of the Exodus, not a ninth Canon spoke. Regions below are the visual bible's geographic names, placed as design-intent sectors inside the 80 km disc. Political `country_id` rows (the Tessera, House Aldermere, the Tally House, the Clearing Spire, and the others) overlap on a local claim plane the way Tunya's circles do. This file does not promote those capitals into kilometres. The Tally House remains a political name with a local nearness that is not the Court.

## Elevation and water

Low and drowned in the south and west of the disc, higher and windier on the highland, industrial-flat on the furnace belt. The Exodus is the outside water. The short strait toward the Iron Coast is about 24 km of open water between disc edges. The long fetch toward the Frontier is the ocean proper. Inland flood is rain and a failed levee, not a clean lake.

## Settlement rules

Build on existing pads: a tally hall, a furnace bay, a pier that still has a bollard, a silo, a headframe. New beauty architecture is the wrong history. Repair is visible. Density follows the furnace shifts and the tally door, and drops to almost nothing on the highland except the thorn and the frame.

## Hazards and edges

Smog is constant midground. Flood is a line on materials. The Mark is a tile, not a damage aura. Contested Passes face Crime. There is no Hub plaque on this continent. A traveler who came by waystone is still on a road, slower, and the stone behind them has no arch.

## Waystone

`env_sere_waystone` on the Crime-facing coast. Brand tile, no portal. See `GATE_AND_ROAD_TABLE.md`.
