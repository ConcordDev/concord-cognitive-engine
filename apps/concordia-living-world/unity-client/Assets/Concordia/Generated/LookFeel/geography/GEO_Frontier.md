# Geography — Frontier

**WorldId:** `Frontier`  
**Center:** (−400 km, 0 km), due west, gate π. Disc 110 km, design-intent.  
**Climate:** Wind and dust. Canon weather is wind. Sky is the largest prop.

## Elevation

Open flats, a drift gap, a dry rut. No placed mesa. The dustgriffin slice says a mesa sentence with no mesa placed; the window uses the drift gap. Do not add a mesa to finish that sentence. Relief is a cut bank and a hand-built bridge, not a mountain wall.

## Hydrology

Dry most of the year. A cottonwood marks where water was. The Walker Paths do not follow a river. Rain, when it comes, is a passing wall, not the Coast's all-day street rain.

## Settlement

Council hitch near the heart. Relay posts and cairns along the roads. Cabins are small and could be taken apart. The Canvas Dive and the Morning Chevron are encounters on `floor_drift_gap`, not towns. The Last Dome's spawn anchor remains "open flat one mile past the frontier gate," and this brief does not convert the mile. The Road That Followed stays between a hitch and a verge until someone places it.

Density: long empty, then a post, then empty. That emptiness is the content. Impostors from the Hub should read as a low line and a mast, not as a city glow.

## Hazards and edges

East is the Crown Road to the Hub. North march to Tunya and south march to the Iron Coast are Walker Paths. West and southwest open into the long Exodus toward Sere, the ocean leg, not a coastal stroll. The refusal of the dome is a placement rule: no energy roof, no enclosed biome bubble. Wind does the work a dome would have refused.

## Roads

Crown Road radial. Walker cairns on the ring marches. Wagon ruts are the local texture. Steel is live. Muay Thai is the stance. Horses are led.
