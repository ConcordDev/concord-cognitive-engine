# Geography — Tunya

**WorldId:** `Tunya`  
**Center:** (−282.84 km, +282.84 km), northwest, gate 3π/4. Disc 110 km, design-intent.  
**Climate:** Many climates on one continent. Canon weather tag is grove, which is the terrace default, not the glacier and not the dune.

Tunya is country-driven. The visual bible names six grammars. Other canon countries inherit the terrace fallback until a profile exists. `countries.json` drops overlapping claim circles of about 18–28 local metres. Those circles are claims. A player standing in two circles is standing in a claim conflict, not on a surveyed border. This brief places six **design-intent sectors** inside the 110 km disc and lists the rest as fallback or unplaced.

## Fallback countries

Inherit terrace timber, limestone, and solar eaves until a dedicated grammar is authored. Do not invent kilometres for them in this pack.

- Dinye, Sahm, Bahiij, Akeia, Vessine have pins in the local claim plane and overlapping radii. Walk the anchors the culture slice already allows. Do not treat the circles as kingdoms you can draw at 400 km scale.
- Corre, Dormas, Vrellan, Sandrun: factions or names with districts and, where the slice says so, no `country_id`. Sandrun shares Sangree's fire-forge look when it is shown at all.
- Named and unplaced: the Hold of First Arrival, the twelfth lip, the sky temple, the quiet quarter, the tide temple, the old Fluxom harbor, the ancestor caves. No coordinate is invented to finish them.

`extends_world` labels on country rows point at other folders. They are labels. Animals and these sectors do not march along that field.

## Elevation and water

Cliffs in Masond, dunes in Asbir, a stack yard in Sangree, ice in Aekon, sea level at Fluxom, a dark basin under Nil's canopy. Terraces between them are the connecting elevation, stepped, with runnels. The Inner Veil touches the Fluxom coast. Inland water is terrace water and the Masond river, not a second ocean.

## Settlement rules

Build on the terrace lip, the cliff landing, the dune shade, the harbor lock, the warm side of the ark rib. Do not reap a terrace into a clearcut. Density follows the terrace roads and the harbor. Nil stays sparse. The glacier hold is a pocket of warmth, not a city.

## Hazards and edges

Southeast of the disc is the Sundering march and the Veil shoal. Southwest is the Frontier march, a Walker Path, drier. The Crown Road leaves the southeast-east side toward the Hub. Forge yards burn scrub on one face only. Dunes bury a door and do not bury the whole sector in a sandstorm VFX.

Steel is live on Tunya ground. A blade carried back onto the Hub plaza, outside the Arena, is a flower.
