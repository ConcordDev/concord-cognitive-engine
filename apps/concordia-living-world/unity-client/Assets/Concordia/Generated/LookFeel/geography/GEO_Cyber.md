# Geography — Cyber, the Grid

**WorldId:** `Cyber`  
**Center:** (+400 km, 0 km), due east, gate angle 0. Disc 110 km, design-intent.  
**Climate:** Haze and wet pavement. Canon weather is neon. The sky is night-biased even at noon.

## Elevation

Stacks are the vertical. Streets are a grid of mid-rise. Under-District is the negative elevation: service corridors and hatches, a few metres down, not a hell level. Sky-bridges are the mid-air roads. The Glitch Chapel is a frame in a block, not a floating island.

## Hydrology

Alleys drain. There is no pastoral river. Water is a trench, a reflection, and the rain that does not turn the lamp into a Coast sodium teardrop. The Uncounted Wharf, on the southwest march toward the Iron Coast, is where this drainage meets a real harbor.

## Settlement

The Stacks and the Under-District are the design-intent pair. Census plinths sit on plazas between stacks. Density is the highest on the ring after the Hub plaza: people, cables, stalls, bridges. Culling is mandatory at L1. Flora is a weed and a moss pad, so do not spend the density budget on trees.

## Hazards and edges

West is the Crown Road home to the Hub. Northwest march is Ruins: acrylic does not replace limestone; the joint weathers. Southwest is the Coast blend. Southeast march to the Crucible is the weak unclosed seam: walkable, unfinished. Identity-as-terrain is not a maze volume. Damage hiding is presentation of hits, not a geographic fog that deletes collisions.
