# Geography — Fantasy, the Sundering

**WorldId:** `Fantasy`  
**Center:** (0 km, +400 km), due north, gate π/2. Disc 110 km, design-intent.  
**Climate:** Temperate forest to alpine. Canon weather clear. Mist is local to waterfalls and the fold.

## Elevation

Three bands inside the disc.

- Wildwood heart, lower, root architecture, the oak and the shrine.
- Fold and ravine, a lip and a drop, mid-disc, where the water falls.
- Alpine rim toward the north edge of the disc, colder, rune-scar pine, thinner soil.

The titan pillar is a landmark height, not a mountain range. House Voss sits on a stair fragment, not on a flying castle.

## Hydrology

One shelf waterfall and a short run of clear water. The salt toll is on the drier road back toward the Crown Road and the Hub bearing (south side of the disc). Do not turn the Sundering into a lake country. The Inner Veil shoal is the south-west coast, toward Tunya, and it is saltier than the fold.

## Settlement

Hearts, not a megacity. Wildwood, House Voss, the Held shrine approach. `countries.json` has claim rows (wildwood circle, House Voss, and several faction countries). Their local circles overlap the way Tunya's do. This atlas pins design-intent sectors only: wildwood west of center, Voss east, shrine toward the north rim. It does not resurvey those claims.

Roads follow the fold's lip and the salt track. Density: travelers on the track, grove quiet off it, alpine almost empty.

## Hazards and edges

South edge meets the Crown Road and, eventually, the Hub approaches along that bearing. Southwest march toward Tunya is Inner Veil water and a road on the dry side. East march toward Ruins is ash-meets-moss: weathering crosses, kits do not merge into a fourth blend. The Held Curse is a boss approach, not a terrain hazard painted on the whole disc.

Steel is live. Flower Law does not govern a ward-blade here.

## Neighbors

Ruins to the southeast, Tunya to the southwest, Hub to the south via the Crown Road.
